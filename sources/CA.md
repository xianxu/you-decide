# California — authoritative sources

For state-level CA races and below. Composes with `sources/US.md` for federal races on CA ballots.

## Tier A — Primary / official

- **CA Secretary of State** (sos.ca.gov) — statewide candidate filings, ballot text, election results
- **CA Legislative Information** (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov) — Assembly/Senate voting records, bill text, committee work
- **CA FPPC** — state-level campaign finance (state Cal-Access at cal-access.sos.ca.gov; local via netfile.com per county)
- **County registrar voter-info tools** — address-to-district lookups, county ballot manifests
  - San Mateo: smcacre.gov
  - Los Angeles: lavote.gov
  - San Francisco: sfelections.org
  - Sacramento: elections.saccounty.gov
  - Santa Clara: sccvote.sccgov.org
  - Alameda: acvote.org
  - San Diego: sdvote.com
  - Orange: ocvote.gov
  - (Add per county as coverage expands)
- **County Office of Education / school-district legistar tools** — local school-district board records

## Tier B — Authoritative-secondary

### Statewide
- **CalMatters** (calmatters.org) — CA nonpartisan policy reporting; deep voter guides
- **KQED** (kqed.org/voterguide) — CA public-radio voter guides
- **LA Times** (latimes.com) — statewide mainstream
- **AP California** — wire coverage of CA races
- **EdSource** (edsource.org) — specialized education reporting; Tier B for education-policy claims

## Tier C — Use with caution

- **Wikipedia** — orienting bio facts only; never decisive
- **Factually.co** — AI aggregator; chase the underlying primary
- **Partisan local outlets** — flag tilt and triangulate

## Specialized

- **Ballotpedia per-jurisdiction-per-year pages** (e.g., `ballotpedia.org/San_Mateo_County,_California,_elections,_2026`) — the most comprehensive non-partisan aggregator for *ballot scoping* (what races, who's running). Tier B for scoping; verify decisive candidate-position claims against primary sources. Same applies state-by-state: see `sources/US.md` for general Ballotpedia notes.
- **Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association** (HJTA-PAC) — useful for fiscal-conservative endorsement tracking
- **CA League of Women Voters** — Smart Voter ballot summaries
- **CA Teachers Association** — endorsement tracking for education-relevant races
- **CCJA / state-court records** — for documented legal controversies
- **CourtListener** — searchable case-law repository
