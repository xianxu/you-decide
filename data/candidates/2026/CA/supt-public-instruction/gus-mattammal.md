# Gus Mattammal — 2026 CA Superintendent of Public Instruction

## Background

Director of Advantage Testing of Silicon Valley, a tutoring and test-prep firm, where he has worked for 22 years; author of *A Is for Average: Why California's Public Schools Struggle, and How You Can Help*. [(EdSource)](https://edsource.org/2026/gus-mattammal-california-superintendent-candidate/756334) Grew up in inner-city St. Louis in a "marginalized, low-income community"; his neighborhood public high school had a low graduation rate, and his parents sacrificed to send him to an all-boys Jesuit school, making him among the few on his block to finish high school and attend college. [(EdSource)](https://edsource.org/2026/gus-mattammal-california-superintendent-candidate/756334) [(Advantage Testing — Gus Mattammal bio)](https://www.advantagetesting.com/tutors/gus-mattammal) Undergraduate in physics and mathematics from Pomona College; MBA (international strategy and finance) from Yale School of Management. [(Advantage Testing — Gus Mattammal bio)](https://www.advantagetesting.com/tutors/gus-mattammal) Has also served as president of SHIFT-Bay Area (housing/infrastructure advocacy) and on the San Mateo County Transportation Authority Community Advisory Committee.

Prior elections: 2022 Republican candidate CA-15 (congressional, did not advance from the primary); 2024 Republican candidate AD-23 (state assembly, lost the March 5, 2024 primary — incumbent Marc Berman and Lydia Kou advanced over Mattammal and Allan Marson). Both lost. ([CA Secretary of State — prior election results](https://electionresults.sos.ca.gov/contests/district/state-assembly); party endorsements detailed under Endorsements below.) The 2026 superintendent filing is NPP — the office is constitutionally nonpartisan, but the Republican electoral history is real.

Campaign raised $83,799 as of March 31, 2026 (small-money campaign; no major institutional money surfaced). ([EdSource](https://edsource.org/2026/gus-mattammal-california-superintendent-candidate/756334))

- **Literacy / science of reading:** Supports the "science of reading" as the correct framework for literacy instruction — a central campaign plank; views the current state approach as underperforming. [(EdSource)](https://edsource.org/2026/gus-mattammal-california-superintendent-candidate/756334)
- **Math:** Calls for content-driven reform in mathematics. [(votegus.org/policy)](https://www.votegus.org/policy)
- **History / ethnic studies:** Includes "history/ethnic studies" in his list of content-driven curriculum reform areas — no detail beyond that; not explicitly antagonistic. [(votegus.org/policy)](https://www.votegus.org/policy)
- **School choice:** Explicitly supports "public charter schools, magnet schools, open enrollment, and microschools" within the public system; would increase CDE support for parents pursuing microschooling or homeschooling. [(votegus.org/policy)](https://www.votegus.org/policy)
- **Education funding / anti-tax-spend:** Proposes a "complete redesign of the supplemental/concentration grant funding system," routing all education-budget growth above inflation to struggling schools — claims this adds $20B "without raising taxes." [(votegus.org/policy)](https://www.votegus.org/policy) Opposes Governor Newsom's proposed shift of superintendent powers to an appointed education commissioner ([EdSource](https://edsource.org/2026/gus-mattammal-california-superintendent-candidate/756334): "hopes the scope of the position isn't altered as proposed by Gov. Gavin Newsom, who wants to shift power to a new education commissioner to be appointed by the next governor").
- **Teacher compensation:** Advocates performance-based pay that rewards "star teachers in struggling districts"; defined-contribution (not defined-benefit) retirement plans for new teachers. [(votegus.org/policy)](https://www.votegus.org/policy)
- **Parental involvement:** Would create a CDE team to "take in observations from parents about happenings within their local schools." [(votegus.org/policy)](https://www.votegus.org/policy)
- **Data-driven posture:** Frames decision-making as data-driven; views the superintendent as an underused change agent; plans to visit districts and use the platform for legislative pressure. [(EdSource — "use data to drive decisions")](https://edsource.org/2026/gus-mattammal-california-superintendent-candidate/756334)
- **No surfaced positions on:** Immigration in schools, gun policy, CRT bans, transgender athlete policy, or 2020 election fraud claims. The office is education-specialist; no Trump-era institutional-violation signals found.
