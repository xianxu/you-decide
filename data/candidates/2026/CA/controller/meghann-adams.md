# Meghann Adams — 2026 CA Controller candidate

## Background

Meghann Adams is the Peace and Freedom Party candidate for California State Controller; her ballot designation is "School Bus Driver." **About the office she seeks:** The California State Controller is the state's chief fiscal officer and accountant — it pays the state's bills, audits state agencies and programs, and reports on the state's finances. CalMatters and LAist describe the Controller as "the state's chief accountant," overseeing spending and payroll in one of the world's largest economies and auditing state finances. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Controller)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/controller/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Controller voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-state-controller) The Controller also sits ex officio on roughly 80 boards and commissions, including state tax boards (such as the Franchise Tax Board) and the large public-pension boards (CalPERS and CalSTRS). It is primarily a technical-administrator office rather than a policy-making one. (inference, from the office's statutory fiscal-officer role.)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. Three candidates are certified: Malia M. Cohen (D, incumbent), Herb W. Morgan (R), and Adams (Peace and Freedom). The top two advance.
