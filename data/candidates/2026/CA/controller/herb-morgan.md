# Herb Morgan — 2026 CA Controller candidate

## Background

Herb W. Morgan is a Republican running for California State Controller; his ballot designation is "Chief Investment Officer." He is a San Diego-based investment-management professional. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Controller)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/controller/) [(KPBS — Controller race explainer, May 4 2026)](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/04/2026-primary-election-california-controller-race-explainer) [(CA Secretary of State — certified candidate list)](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf)

Per his campaign's own bio (self-report), Morgan founded Efficient Market Advisors, an ETF-focused registered investment adviser, in 2004, serving as CEO and Chief Investment Officer until Cantor Fitzgerald acquired the firm in 2017; he then became a Senior Managing Director and CIO of Cantor's managed-ETF portfolios. **About the office he seeks:** The California State Controller is the state's chief fiscal officer and accountant — it pays the state's bills, audits state agencies and programs, and reports on the state's finances. CalMatters and LAist describe the Controller as "the state's chief accountant," overseeing spending and payroll in one of the world's largest economies and auditing state finances. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Controller)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/controller/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Controller voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-state-controller) The Controller also sits ex officio on roughly 80 boards and commissions, including state tax boards (such as the Franchise Tax Board) and the large public-pension boards (CalPERS and CalSTRS). It is primarily a technical-administrator office rather than a policy-making one. (inference, from the office's statutory fiscal-officer role.)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. Three candidates are certified: Malia M. Cohen (D, incumbent), Morgan (R), and Meghann Adams (Peace and Freedom). The top two advance. [(CA Secretary of State — certified candidate list)](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf) [(Ballotpedia — California Controller election, 2026)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_Controller_election,_2026)
