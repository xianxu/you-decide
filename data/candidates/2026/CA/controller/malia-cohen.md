# Malia Cohen — 2026 CA Controller candidate

## Background

Malia M. Cohen is a Democrat and California's incumbent State Controller, first elected in 2022 (defeating Republican Lanhee Chen) and running for a second term; her ballot designation is "State Controller/Mother." She is **not** term-limited out of this office. [(CalMatters — 2026 Controller race, May 8 2026)](https://calmatters.org/politics/elections/2026/05/ca-election-2026-controller/) [(KPBS — Controller race explainer, May 4 2026)](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/04/2026-primary-election-california-controller-race-explainer) [(Ballotpedia — California Controller election, 2026)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_Controller_election,_2026)

Her educational background is in public policy and administration rather than accounting: a BA from Fisk University and a master's in public policy and management from Carnegie Mellon University. (Orienting bio — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malia_Cohen), orienting-only.) No accounting credential (e.g., CPA) surfaced for Cohen in this research pass, and no source claims one; she is best described as a politician/public administrator by background. (inference, absence-of-evidence — not a positive finding that she lacks relevant competence.)

Before the Controller's office she served on the San Francisco Board of Supervisors (2011–2019) and was elected to the California Board of Equalization in 2018, chairing it in 2019 and 2022; in that role she helped oversee the distribution of roughly $80 billion in tax revenue. [(CalMatters — Cohen profile, May 2022)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2022/05/malia-cohen-controller/)

**About the office she seeks:** The California State Controller is the state's chief fiscal officer and accountant — it pays the state's bills, audits state agencies and programs, and reports on the state's finances. CalMatters and LAist describe the Controller as "the state's chief accountant," overseeing spending and payroll in one of the world's largest economies and auditing state finances. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Controller)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/controller/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Controller voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-state-controller) The Controller also sits ex officio on roughly 80 boards and commissions, including state tax boards (such as the Franchise Tax Board) and the large public-pension boards (CalPERS and CalSTRS). It is primarily a technical-administrator office rather than a policy-making one. (inference, from the office's statutory fiscal-officer role.)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. Three candidates are certified: Cohen (D, incumbent), Herb W. Morgan (R), and Meghann Adams (Peace and Freedom). The top two advance. [(CA Secretary of State — certified candidate list)](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf) [(Ballotpedia — California Controller election, 2026)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_Controller_election,_2026)

## Record / past performance

**Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR) turnaround:**
- Cohen's office published the FY2024–25 ACFR on what it calls "the fastest turnaround on record for a financial report of California's size and complexity," completed "just eight months after the prior report" and "returned to its Spring publication target." The release states that "for the first time in eight years," lawmakers, taxpayers, bond markets, and the public would have timely audited financial information before major budget decisions were finalized. [(SCO press release — ACFR)](https://www.sco.ca.gov/eo_pressrel_27489.html) [(Lassen News — Controller unveils California's audited financial reality)](https://www.lassennews.com/controller-unveils-californias-audited-financial-reality-ahead-of-governors-may-revise) Note: the "fastest turnaround on record" framing originates with her own office and has not been independently audited; treat the superlative as her office's claim. (inference.)
- Context for the "eight years" figure: California had a multi-year streak of late audited financial statements predating her tenure, documented by an outside watchdog. [(California Policy Center — California's streak of late financial statements continues)](https://californiapolicycenter.org/californias-streak-of-late-financial-statement-continues/) (Tier C, watchdog/advocacy — used only to corroborate the *existence* of a years-long late-statements problem, not to credit or discredit Cohen.)
