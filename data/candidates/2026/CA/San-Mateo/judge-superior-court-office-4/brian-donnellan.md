# Brian Donnellan — 2026 SM County Superior Court Judge, Office No. 4 candidate

## Background

**About the office.** "Judge of the Superior Court, Office No. 4" is a San Mateo County trial-court seat — a single non-partisan judgeship decided countywide. This is an **open seat** created by the retirement of Judge Susan Greenberg, and it is the **first contested San Mateo County Superior Court election since 2014**. Two candidates appear on the ballot, both career prosecutors: San Mateo County Deputy District Attorney Brian Donnellan and Santa Clara County's second-in-command prosecutor Jay Boyarsky. ([SM County Registrar candidate roster](https://smcacre.gov/system/files/2026-02/51_candidateroster02192026.pdf) [Tier A]; [The Almanac, 2026-05-28](https://www.almanacnews.com/san-mateo-county/2026/05/28/voters-get-a-say-in-san-mateo-countys-next-judge/) [Tier B])

Neither candidate is a sitting judge, so there is **no judicial record to assess** for either — the comparison rests on prosecutorial career, reputation, and endorsements. Additionally, **no formal county-bar "qualified"/"well-qualified" judicial evaluation rating was found for either candidate.** `DATA-GAP [axis: judicial qualification rating; severity: low; last-attempt: 2026-06-02]` — no published San Mateo County Bar Association (or equivalent) candidate evaluation surfaced for the Office No. 4 race.

**Donnellan biography.** Born in Ireland and brought to the United States as an infant, Donnellan was raised in San Mateo County. He earned a bachelor's degree and a law degree, both from the University of San Francisco. Before law he spent roughly six years in the airline industry, and he served 24 years in the U.S. Army Reserve, deploying to Iraq in 2004 and earning a Bronze Star. ([Palo Alto Online, 2026-02-18](https://www.paloaltoonline.com/courts/2026/02/18/veteran-prosecutors-vie-for-rare-judge-opening-in-san-mateo-county/) [Tier B]; [SM Daily Journal](https://www.smdailyjournal.com/news/local/2-candidates-vie-for-san-mateo-county-judge-position-san-mateo-county-deputy-district-attorney/article_c1858048-05cc-457f-96b3-afbe076a1447.html) [Tier B]; [donnellanforjudge.com](https://www.donnellanforjudge.com) [Tier A])
