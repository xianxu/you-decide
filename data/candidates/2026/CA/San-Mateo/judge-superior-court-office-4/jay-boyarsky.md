# Jay Boyarsky — 2026 SM County Superior Court Judge, Office No. 4 candidate

## Background

**About the office.** "Judge of the Superior Court, Office No. 4" is a San Mateo County trial-court seat — a single non-partisan judgeship decided countywide. This is an **open seat** created by the retirement of Judge Susan Greenberg, and it is the **first contested San Mateo County Superior Court election since 2014**. Two candidates appear on the ballot, both career prosecutors: San Mateo County Deputy District Attorney Brian Donnellan and Santa Clara County's second-in-command prosecutor Jay Boyarsky. ([SM County Registrar candidate roster](https://smcacre.gov/system/files/2026-02/51_candidateroster02192026.pdf) [Tier A]; [The Almanac, 2026-05-28](https://www.almanacnews.com/san-mateo-county/2026/05/28/voters-get-a-say-in-san-mateo-countys-next-judge/) [Tier B])

Neither candidate is a sitting judge, so there is **no judicial record to assess** for either — the comparison rests on prosecutorial career, reputation, and endorsements.
