# Héctor Camacho Jr — 2026 SM County Superintendent of Schools

## Background

26-year career in K-12 education: began as a bilingual instructional aide, then moved through classroom teaching, school counseling, and administrative roles. Served 11 years as a San Mateo County Board of Education trustee (governance, not operations). Appointed Executive Director of Equity, Social Justice and Inclusion at SMCOE in January 2025 — only ~17 months in that role before launching this campaign. Holds a valid California Administrative Services Credential (legally required for the office). South San Francisco-based.

Opponent: Chelsea Bonini (SMCOE Board trustee).

- **Community outreach:** Wants SMCOE services to go to under-resourced districts rather than requiring districts to come to the county office — operational improvement, not a policy position.
