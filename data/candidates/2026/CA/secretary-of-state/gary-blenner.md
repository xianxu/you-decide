# Gary Blenner — 2026 CA Secretary of State candidate

## Background

Gary Blenner is the Green Party of California's statewide-endorsed candidate for Secretary of State, running on the GPCA's "Left Unity Slate." His ballot designation / occupation in this race is "Teacher." [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-secretary-of-state) [(GPCA — Greens in June 2026)](https://cagreens.org/elections/greens_june_2026)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. The field has four candidates: incumbent Democrat Shirley Weber, Republican Don Wagner, and two Green Party candidates — Blenner and Michael Feinstein. The top two advance. [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-secretary-of-state) Of the two Greens, Blenner carries the statewide GPCA endorsement (Feinstein has only a Los Angeles County Green endorsement). [(GPCA — Greens in June 2026)](https://cagreens.org/elections/greens_june_2026)

Blenner previously ran for this same office in 2022, also on the Green/Peace-and-Freedom "Left Unity Slate." [(GPCA — endorsed 2022 statewide candidates)](https://www.cagreens.org/endorsed-2022-statewide-candidates) (Orienting bio — broader personal/career background beyond "Teacher" was not corroborated to a Tier-A/B source in this pass; see DATA-GAP under Record.)

**About the office he seeks:** The California Secretary of State is the state's chief elections officer — the office "oversees the administration of all federal and state elections in California, including certifying candidates and initiatives for statewide ballots, issuing voter guides and ensuring votes are properly counted" — and its work also spans corporate business filings, campaign-finance and lobbying disclosure, and the state archives. The office has limited independent policy power: the structural reforms Blenner runs on — proportional representation and ranked-choice voting — cannot be adopted unilaterally by the Secretary; they require the Legislature or statewide ballot measures. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/secretary-of-state/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-secretary-of-state) His "expand ballot access" plank is closer to the office's actual administrative remit, but the proportional-representation/RCV core is a legislative-or-ballot question, not a Secretary-of-State lever.
