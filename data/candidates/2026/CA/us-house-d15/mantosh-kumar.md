# Mantosh Kumar — 2026 US House CA-D15

## Background

Age 53, San Mateo resident for 25+ years. Electrical Engineering degree + MBA. Career in Silicon Valley corporate roles: Oracle (product engineering), Cisco (strategy/finance/execution), ServiceNow (strategy/operations). Claims management of programs valued $100M–$1B+. No prior elected or appointed office — first-time candidate. No documented civic leadership roles (boards, commissions, community orgs) surfaced in research. Wife works for the University of California. Certified on the 2026 ballot as "Mantosh Kumar — Democratic, Strategic Business Advisor" ([CA SoS Certified List of Candidates, June 2 2026 primary](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf)).

Motivation for primarying Mullin: frustration that incumbent Democrats are "lying down — hoping that people's outrage will propel them to power" and insufficient action on housing, healthcare, corporate capture. Specifically criticizes Mullin's support for Prop 19 ("which stripped inheritance protections from working families and seniors") and an "Empty Lot" bill that "reads like it was written by developer lobbyists" ([mantosh.us](https://mantosh.us/), re-fetched live 2026-05-29). Also cites Mullin's AIPAC-linked donations as evidence of capture — Kumar states: "Kevin Mullin takes lots of AIPAC money, $862,000 to be precise (Source: Track AIPAC). I do not." ([Patch](https://patch.com/california/redwoodcity-woodside/meet-mantosh-kumar-candidate-ca-congress)). **DATA-FIXME** [axis: capture-risk; severity: low; last-attempt: 2026-05-29]: The $862K figure is Kumar's characterization; Track AIPAC's own tally for Mullin is ~$743K ($125,185 direct + $618,681 independent expenditures), and Mullin says he has taken no AIPAC money this cycle. Reported here as Kumar's claim, not as established fact.

## Stated positions

All from campaign site [mantosh.us](https://mantosh.us/) and Patch/Almanac/SM Daily Journal coverage unless noted.

**Israel/Gaza**: States "What is happening in Gaza is a genocide. I will say it. Over 50,000 Palestinians have been killed — the majority women, children, and elderly civilians — since October 2023." Would condition U.S. military aid to Israel on compliance with international humanitarian law; distinguishes between the Jewish people and the Israeli government's conduct. Identifies Gaza as one of two core differences with Mullin (the other being campaign finance). [(Patch — genocide / "over 50,000" quote verified verbatim 2026-05-29)](https://patch.com/california/redwoodcity-woodside/meet-mantosh-kumar-candidate-ca-congress)

**Immigration**: Vague on specifics. Opposes "secret-police tactics" and "politics of fear." Advocates due process protections for immigrants and "a fair, orderly, and humane immigration system." No detail on enforcement, pathways, or illegal-entry disincentives. [(Patch)](https://patch.com/california/redwoodcity-woodside/meet-mantosh-kumar-candidate-ca-congress)

**Federal taxes/fiscal**: Medicare for All (single-payer covering medical, dental, vision, mental health). Repeal Trump tariffs. Advocates "One Percent Bill" and antitrust enforcement against price coordination. Federal renter credits accounting for indirect property tax payments. No mention of federal deficit, spending discipline, or revenue side. [(Almanac)](https://www.almanacnews.com/redwood-city/2026/05/18/five-candidates-up-to-bat-for-congressional-d-15/)

**AI/labor**: Proposes AI displacement tax, transparency and use mandates, environmental hard-caps on AI infrastructure (data centers). References alignment with Bernie Sanders on AI worker protections. AI training programs in schools and community labs. [(Almanac)](https://www.almanacnews.com/redwood-city/2026/05/18/five-candidates-up-to-bat-for-congressional-d-15/)

**Housing**: "Rational zoning laws" to promote housing production; transit-connected affordable housing; property tax circuit-breakers; expanded housing vouchers; federal renter credits. Criticizes Prop 19 (inheritance tax) and "Empty Lot" bill as real-estate-industry wins. [(SM Daily Journal)](https://www.smdailyjournal.com/news/local/2-democrats-to-challenge-mullin/article_791ef8bb-e895-4ef2-b988-5c49b66a334a.html)

**Healthcare**: Medicare for All — comprehensive single-payer. Accountability for insurance companies and county boards raising premiums. [(Almanac)](https://www.almanacnews.com/redwood-city/2026/05/18/five-candidates-up-to-bat-for-congressional-d-15/)

**Energy**: Criticizes PG&E rates — San Mateo County CA-15 families "pay approximately 43 cents per kilowatt-hour," vs. "roughly 20 cents" in Palo Alto and "near 13 cents" in Sacramento. Promotes community-choice energy and public-ownership consideration. [(Patch — figures verified verbatim 2026-05-29)](https://patch.com/california/redwoodcity-woodside/meet-mantosh-kumar-candidate-ca-congress)

**Campaign finance**: "No corporate PAC money. No special interest strings." Individual donations via ActBlue only. Frames this as core differentiator from Mullin. [(mantosh.us, re-fetched live 2026-05-29)](https://mantosh.us/)
