# Fiona Ma — 2026 CA Lieutenant Governor candidate

## Background

Fiona Ma is a Democrat and California's State Treasurer, in office since January 2019. She is a Certified Public Accountant (CPA), and that financial-professional credential anchors her "knows the budget" public brand. She is married to a firefighter, and the daughter-of-immigrants framing recurs in her messaging. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(fionama.com)](https://fionama.com/why-i-am-running/)

Her career has moved up the California ladder of fiscal and elected offices: San Francisco Supervisor, then four terms in the California State Assembly (2006–2014) — where she rose to Speaker pro Tempore / Assistant Speaker and served on the Assembly Public Safety Committee — then a seat on the California Board of Equalization (2014–2018), and since 2019 the statewide office of State Treasurer. [(CalMatters race story)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/06/california-primary-lieutenant-governor/) [(OpenCampaign)](https://www.opencampaign.com/politicians-in-united-states/163800/fiona-ma/issue-positions) [(treasurer.ca.gov)](https://www.treasurer.ca.gov/)

As of the June 2, 2026 top-two primary she is the clear Democratic frontrunner in the lieutenant governor field, leading on name recognition, money, and labor/party backing. [(CalMatters race story)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/06/california-primary-lieutenant-governor/)

**About the office she seeks:** The California Lieutenant Governor has limited independent power. The office's substantive roles are mostly ex-officio board seats — Regent of the University of California, Trustee of the California State University, and member (and chair) of the State Lands Commission — plus serving as President of the State Senate with a tie-breaking vote, and standing first in line of succession to the Governor. Most of Ma's stated "plans" below would, in practice, depend on the Governor, Legislature, or her current/other offices rather than on the lieutenant governorship itself. (inference)

## Controversies / liabilities

**1. Blackwell sexual-harassment lawsuit → 2024 state settlement (most serious):**
Judith Blackwell, a former senior official whom Ma hired and later fired, filed suit in 2021 alleging that Ma exposed herself and climbed into bed with Blackwell during work-related travel, along with racial discrimination and wrongful / retaliatory termination. In 2023, Sacramento Superior Court Judge Christopher Krueger dismissed the racial-discrimination and wrongful-termination claims but allowed the **sexual-harassment claim to proceed to trial.** The State of California then **settled for $350,000**, with the settlement filed August 25, 2024 (shortly before a September 2024 trial). Taxpayers paid the settlement, and there was **no admission of liability** and no court finding of wrongdoing — the matter was settled, not adjudicated. Ma has called the suit a "frivolous lawsuit" with "fabricated claims" and characterized the settlement as "a vindication." [(CBS Sacramento — settlement, Aug 2024)](https://www.cbsnews.com/sacramento/news/california-settles-sexual-harassment-claims-against-state-treasurer-fiona-ma/) [(CBS Sacramento — claim cleared for trial, 2023)](https://www.cbsnews.com/sacramento/news/judge-sexual-harassment-lawsuit-against-california-treasurer-by-employee-she-fired-can-go-to-trial/)

**2. FPPC stipulated agreement (campaign-finance reporting):**
In *In re Fiona Ma for State Treasurer 2018*, FPPC Case No. 22/195, Ma's 2018 campaign committee entered a stipulated agreement in January 2024, arising from a Franchise Tax Board audit. The three counts were: failure to timely disclose / itemize subvendor payments, failure to maintain subvendor records, and failure to timely disclose credit-card charges. The total fine was **$11,500.** [(FPPC stipulation, Jan 2024)](https://www.fppc.ca.gov/enforcement/EnfDivCaseResults/stipulated-agreements/2024-sdo/january-sdo/fiona-ma-for-state-treasurer-2018.html)
