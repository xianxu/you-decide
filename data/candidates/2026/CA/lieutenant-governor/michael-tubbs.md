# Michael Tubbs — 2026 CA Lieutenant Governor candidate

## Background

Michael Tubbs is a Democrat and the former Mayor of Stockton, California (2017–2021) — Stockton's first Black mayor, and elected at 26. He took office years after Stockton's 2012 municipal bankruptcy and ran on economic-mobility and anti-poverty themes throughout his public career. [(CalMatters 2026 voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(SoS voter guide)](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/candidates/lt-governor-candidate-statements.htm)

He holds a BA and MA from Stanford University, has held fellowships at the Harvard Institute of Politics and the MIT Media Lab, and is a member of the American Academy of Arts & Sciences. [(amacad.org)](https://www.amacad.org/person/michael-d-tubbs)

He founded several institutions: Mayors for a Guaranteed Income (MGI), a national coalition the campaign says has been emulated by 150+ cities; Stockton Scholars, a place-based college-scholarship program; and Stockton's Office of Violence Prevention. He is the author of the memoir *The Deeper the Roots* (Flatiron, an Oprah Book, November 2021). In 2021 he was named a special adviser to Governor Newsom on economic mobility. [(CalMatters — guaranteed income)](https://calmatters.org/california-divide/2023/04/california-guaranteed-income/) [(Stockton Scholars)](https://stocktonscholars.org/) [(michaeltubbsforca.com)](https://michaeltubbsforca.com/)

As of the June 2, 2026 top-two primary he runs a progressive / grassroots lane and trails frontrunner Fiona Ma on money and establishment endorsements. [(CalMatters race story)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/06/california-primary-lieutenant-governor/)

**About the office he seeks:** The California Lieutenant Governor has limited independent power. The office's substantive roles are mostly ex-officio board seats — Regent of the University of California, Trustee of the California State University, and member (and chair) of the State Lands Commission — plus serving as President of the State Senate with a tie-breaking vote, and standing first in line of succession to the Governor. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-lieutenant-governor) Several of Tubbs's stated "plans" below are framed explicitly around these seat-authorities (public-land housing, offshore wind), while others (guaranteed income, tuition policy) depend on the Governor, Legislature, or the Regents/Trustees as a body rather than on the lieutenant governorship alone. **Energy:**

## Record / past performance

**As Mayor of Stockton (2017–2021):**
- Took office years after Stockton's 2012 bankruptcy and led a post-bankruptcy city; his "builder" record is institution-building (programs), not hard infrastructure or deregulation. (inference)
- Stood up an Office of Violence Prevention and used the Advance Peace model and focused deterrence; homicides fell over his term per independent counts (see public-safety caveat above). [(ABC10)](https://www.abc10.com/article/news/local/crime-is-down-in-stockton-despite-recent-spike-in-homicides/103-454758032)
- Launched the **Stockton Economic Empowerment Demonstration (SEED)**, a guaranteed-income pilot: $500/month for 24 months to 125 residents, 2019–2021. SEED was **privately / philanthropically funded** (including the Economic Security Project), **not taxpayer-funded.** [(stocktondemonstration.org)](https://www.stocktondemonstration.org/) [(CalMatters — guaranteed income)](https://calmatters.org/california-divide/2023/04/california-guaranteed-income/)
- **SEED results nuance:** an early first-year report showed full-time employment rising from 28% to 40%, but the **final study (2023; researchers Amy Castro and Stacia West) found the employment effects were not statistically significant**, with the pandemic disrupting year two. The robust findings were reduced income volatility, lower depression and anxiety, and improved well-being. Researcher Amy Castro: "$500 a month is not a panacea." [(stocktondemonstration.org)](https://www.stocktondemonstration.org/) [(CalMatters — guaranteed income)](https://calmatters.org/california-divide/2023/04/california-guaranteed-income/)

**Stockton Scholars (founded 2018):**
- Place-based college-promise scholarship: up to $4,000 over four years (or $1,000 for community college / trade), with a low 2.0-GPA barrier, plus mentoring and persistence support. Campaign/program figures: roughly 5,000 scholarships and more than $3 million awarded, with the first cohort graduated. [(Stockton Scholars)](https://stocktonscholars.org/) [(PRNewswire — Stockton Scholars first grads, 2023)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lower-barriers-to-higher-ed-pay-off-as-stockton-scholars-celebrates-first-class-of-college-grads-301847604.html)

**National policy movement:**
- Founded Mayors for a Guaranteed Income (MGI), which he says has been emulated by 150+ cities, and helped run an RCT-style pilot (SEED) with an academic evaluation — a scaled, evidence-oriented policy effort. [(CalMatters — guaranteed income)](https://calmatters.org/california-divide/2023/04/california-guaranteed-income/) [(amacad.org)](https://www.amacad.org/person/michael-d-tubbs)

**Electoral record / institutionalism:**
- Won the Stockton mayoralty in 2016 with 70%+, then **lost reelection on November 3, 2020 to Republican Kevin Lincoln by more than 10 points.** He conceded normally; no election-denial. [(CalMatters commentary, Dec 2020)](https://calmatters.org/commentary/2020/12/california-mayors-election-lose/)
- During the 2020 campaign, the local blog 209 Times ran articles — characterized by CalMatters as "often with no proof" — alleging misappropriation and self-dealing. **No charges and no findings resulted**; the allegations are unsubstantiated, and CalMatters framed them as disinformation. No proven self-dealing exists on the record. [(CalMatters commentary, Dec 2020)](https://calmatters.org/commentary/2020/12/california-mayors-election-lose/)

**Note on campaign-sourced statistics:** several headline figures — the "40% homicide reduction," "second most fiscally healthy city," and a "60% unemployment drop" framing — are campaign-sourced and/or period-stretched and should be read as such rather than as independently verified outcomes. ## Controversies / liabilities

**1. 2020 self-dealing allegations (unsubstantiated):**
During his 2020 reelection campaign, the local blog 209 Times published articles — described by CalMatters as "often with no proof" — alleging misappropriation and self-dealing. **No charges were filed and no findings were made.** The allegations remain unsubstantiated, and CalMatters characterized them as disinformation. There is no proven self-dealing on the record. [(CalMatters commentary, Dec 2020)](https://calmatters.org/commentary/2020/12/california-mayors-election-lose/)

**2. Advance Peace criticism:**
Stockton's use of the Advance Peace model — which pays cash stipends to individuals at highest risk of gun violence, some with gun-crime records, paired with mentorship — drew local criticism (e.g., 209 Times, 2018) over the idea of paying people with criminal histories. [(ABC10)](https://www.abc10.com/article/news/local/crime-is-down-in-stockton-despite-recent-spike-in-homicides/103-454758032)

**3. Campaign-sourced / period-stretched statistics:**
The "40% homicide reduction," "second most fiscally healthy city," and "60% unemployment drop" figures are campaign-sourced and, in the homicide case, appear to span his term and successor years. They should be treated as campaign claims, not independently verified outcomes. (inference) [(SoS voter guide)](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/candidates/lt-governor-candidate-statements.htm) [(ABC10)](https://www.abc10.com/article/news/local/crime-is-down-in-stockton-despite-recent-spike-in-homicides/103-454758032)

**4. Guaranteed-income evidence gap:**
His signature policy's flagship pilot (SEED) produced robust well-being and income-volatility findings, but its widely cited early employment effect (28%→40%) did **not** hold as statistically significant in the final study. Opponents could argue the headline employment claim outran the evidence. [(stocktondemonstration.org)](https://www.stocktondemonstration.org/) [(CalMatters — guaranteed income)](https://calmatters.org/california-divide/2023/04/california-guaranteed-income/)

**5. Viability:**
Trails on cash and establishment endorsements and sits at ~8% (third) in early polling, in a tight contest with Fryday for the second Democratic-friendly top-two slot. Plausible but not favored to advance. [(CalMatters race story)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/06/california-primary-lieutenant-governor/) [(CEPP / Cal Poly Pomona)](https://www.cpp.edu/class/political-science/)
