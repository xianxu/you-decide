# Josh Fryday — 2026 CA Lieutenant Governor candidate

*Context on the office:* California's lieutenant governor has limited independent power. The role's documented levers are a seat on the **UC Board of Regents**, a seat on the **CSU Board of Trustees**, membership on the **State Lands Commission** (which controls state-owned land and tidelands), chairing the **Commission for Economic Development**, casting tie-breaking votes in the **State Senate**, and **succeeding the governor**. Fryday's platform is unusually tied to these statutory tools (housing on Regents/Trustees/State Lands land; reviving the dormant Commission for Economic Development), which is why the office's real powers are flagged throughout this dossier.

## Background

Josh Fryday (born February 8, 1981, in Novato, CA) earned a BA from UC Berkeley (2003) and a JD from UC Berkeley Law (2009). During law school he clerked at the US Attorney's Office and at the San Francisco District Attorney's office under then-DA Kamala Harris ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Fryday); [joshfryday.com/about](https://joshfryday.com/about)).

He served in the **US Navy JAG Corps as a lieutenant from 2009 to 2013**, including work on the Guantanamo Bay military commissions and on 2011 Japan tsunami / Fukushima relief efforts ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Fryday)).

In local politics he served on the **Novato City Council from 2015 to 2019**, was **Mayor in 2018**, and resigned in August 2019. Before his current state role he was **Chief Operating Officer of NextGen America**, Tom Steyer's climate organization ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Fryday)).

Since September 2019 he has served as **Chief Service Officer heading California Volunteers**, the state office within Gov. Gavin Newsom's administration that runs California's service programs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Fryday); [californiavolunteers.ca.gov](https://www.californiavolunteers.ca.gov/)).

He is married to a public-school teacher, has three sons, lives in Davis, and is Jewish ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Fryday); [joshfryday.com/about](https://joshfryday.com/about)).

This is a **top-two primary on June 2, 2026**; Fryday is a Democrat.

- Endorses **CEQA reform**, "cutting permitting timelines, reducing barriers to infill and mixed-use development," and fast-tracking/streamlining approvals ([joshfryday.com/issue](https://joshfryday.com/issue); [GrowSF voter guide](https://growsf.org/voter-guide/))
- Sets a target of **1 million units committed/permitted on public and campus land, tracked publicly** — and would use the lieutenant governor's **UC Regents / CSU Trustees seats and the State Lands Commission** to release surplus state parcels near transit (which maps the proposal directly onto the office's actual statutory tools) ([GrowSF questionnaire](https://growsf.org/voter-guide/); [joshfryday.com/issue](https://joshfryday.com/issue))

## Controversies / liabilities

**"Newsom loyalist" / establishment framing:** Fryday is openly Newsom's handpicked candidate; his current job sits inside Newsom's office, and the record he runs on (California Volunteers) is a Newsom initiative. Reporting casts him as an "establishment moderate with gubernatorial backing." No documented self-dealing — but the entanglement is itself a liability for voters wary of insider succession ([Press Democrat, May 10 2026](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/)).

**Funding-detail gap:** The Press Democrat (May 10 2026) noted he gave no detail on how he would fund his universal-service proposals — a recurring critique that his service-expansion ideas are costed thinly ([Press Democrat, May 10 2026](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/)).

**Public-safety documentation gap:** Lists "reducing crime" and "cleaning up homelessness" as goals with no substantive plan, no Prop 47/36 stance, and no mental-health specifics ([joshfryday.com/issue](https://joshfryday.com/issue)).

**Minor:** 2015 Novato council race drew criticism for out-of-county donations ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Fryday)).

**Low name recognition / viability:** The race is wide-open with low name ID — roughly 9 in 10 voters have no opinion ([joshfryday.com/polling](https://joshfryday.com/polling); [CalMatters, Jun 2 2026](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/06/california-primary-lieutenant-governor/)).
- **David Binder Research** (campaign-commissioned, Feb 19 2026): after a balanced bio read, among non-Republicans Fryday 14% / Fiona Ma 14% (tied) / Michael Tubbs 11% ([joshfryday.com/polling](https://joshfryday.com/polling))
- **CEPP / Cal Poly Pomona** (independent, Jun 1 2026): Tubbs 8%, Fryday 7%, with Ma 15% leading and Republican Romero 11% ([CEPP poll](https://www.cpp.edu/class/political-science/))
- A genuine contender on money + Newsom + bio movement, but the one **independent** poll has him at 7% with no lead; his path depends on a TV buy converting his bio advantage into votes.
