# Gloria Romero — 2026 CA Lieutenant Governor candidate

## Background

Gloria Romero is a Republican, a former Democratic state legislative leader, and a longtime education-reform advocate. CalMatters describes her as a "former longtime Democratic lawmaker who served nearly 12 years in the state Legislature." Her campaign biography states she "earned a PhD from the University of California, Riverside" and served "as a professor at Cal State Los Angeles, where she was named Professor Emeritus." [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(gloriaromero4ltgov.com)](https://gloriaromero4ltgov.com/)

Her elected career was in the California Legislature as a Democrat. Per CalMatters, "from 2005 to 2008, Romero was Senate majority leader, the first woman to serve in that role." She chaired the Senate Education Committee, and is the most legislatively experienced candidate in the lieutenant governor field. (Wikipedia adds the office detail — Assembly 1998–2001, Senate 2001–2010 in the 24th district covering East LA and the San Gabriel Valley — as orienting-only context.) [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(Pepperdine School of Public Policy — faculty bio)](https://publicpolicy.pepperdine.edu/academics/faculty/gloria-romero/) [(Ballotpedia — race)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026) [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician))

She switched her registration to Republican in 2024 — CalMatters confirms she "became a registered Republican in 2024 after years of expressing discontent with the Democratic Party," framing the move as a working-class realignment. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/)

As of the June 2, 2026 top-two primary she is the California Republican Party–endorsed candidate and carries the highest GOP name recognition and experience in the field. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(Ballotpedia — race)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026)

**About the office she seeks:** The California Lieutenant Governor has limited independent power. The office's substantive roles are mostly ex-officio board seats — Regent of the University of California, Trustee of the California State University, and member (and chair) of the State Lands Commission — plus serving as President of the State Senate with a tie-breaking vote, and standing first in line of succession to the Governor. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-lieutenant-governor) The UC Regent and CSU Trustee seats are the most direct levers for her education-reform agenda; most other "plans" below would depend on the Governor, Legislature, or other offices. ## Record / past performance

**Education reform (signature record):**
- Authored the Parent Empowerment Act, the "Parent Trigger" law (SBX5 4, 2010, with Sen. Bob Huff; signed by Gov. Schwarzenegger; passed by one vote in each house) — the first such law in the nation. It lets a majority of parents at a chronically failing school force restructuring. Romero, in her own words: "I am the author of the original parent trigger law in the nation, which today has seen it go across the nation to some 20 states having this concept introduced into potential law." [(EdWeek — "Why I Wrote a Parent-Trigger Law," 2013)](https://www.edweek.org/leadership/opinion-why-i-wrote-a-parent-trigger-law/2013/11) [(NPR StateImpact — Q&A with Romero, 2012)](https://stateimpact.npr.org/florida/2012/02/02/q-a-gloria-romero-author-of-californias-parent-trigger-law/)
- Also authored the Open Enrollment Act. [(EdWeek)](https://www.edweek.org/leadership/opinion-why-i-wrote-a-parent-trigger-law/2013/11)
- California Director of Democrats for Education Reform and founder/strategic planner of the California Center for Parent Empowerment. [(Pepperdine School of Public Policy — faculty bio)](https://publicpolicy.pepperdine.edu/academics/faculty/gloria-romero/)
- Founder and Executive Director of Scholarship Prep Charter School (Santa Ana, a countywide-benefit TK–8 charter with a high-poverty / foster-youth focus). [(Pepperdine School of Public Policy — faculty bio)](https://publicpolicy.pepperdine.edu/academics/faculty/gloria-romero/)

**Legislative leadership:**
- First woman California Senate Majority Leader (2005–2008); chaired the Senate Education Committee. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(Pepperdine School of Public Policy — faculty bio)](https://publicpolicy.pepperdine.edu/academics/faculty/gloria-romero/)

**Party switch and consistency:**
- Switched to Republican in 2024 (fact confirmed by CalMatters; see Background), which frames the move as a working-class realignment. CalMatters corroborates the general areas of difference driving the switch: school choice, COVID-19 policies, and gender identity. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/)

**2. "Golden Ticket" alliance — campaign branding, not a joint ticket:**
Romero has a voluntary campaign alliance with GOP gubernatorial candidate Steve Hilton, branded "California's Golden Ticket." To be clear, California elects its Governor and Lieutenant Governor **separately, not as a joint ticket** — the alliance is campaign branding and coordination, not a constitutional pairing. Voters cast independent votes for each office, and the two could in principle win or lose independently of each other. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(Santa Barbara Independent — LG profile, May 2026)](https://www.independent.com/2026/05/05/meet-the-candidates-for-california-lieutenant-governor-a-job-about-nothing/)

**3. 2021 Larry Elder endorsement (coherence note):**
In the 2021 recall she broke party ranks to endorse Larry Elder on the issue of school choice, characterized as an anti-Newsom tactical vote — the main coherence ding against her otherwise consistent record, since she has elsewhere kept her distance from Elder. The endorsement is corroborated by national reporting (Newsweek, Washington Times, Fox News, Aug 2021), which identified her as the highest-profile Democrat to back Elder over education / charter schools.
