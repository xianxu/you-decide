# Gloria Romero — 2026 CA Lieutenant Governor candidate

## Background

Gloria Romero is a Republican, a former Democratic state legislative leader, and a longtime education-reform advocate. She holds a Ph.D. from UC Riverside and is a Professor Emeritus of psychology at Cal State LA. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician)) [(Ballotpedia — race)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026)

Her elected career was in the California Legislature as a Democrat: the State Assembly (1998–2001) and the State Senate (2001–2010, 24th district covering East LA and the San Gabriel Valley). She chaired the Senate Education Committee and in 2005 became the first woman to serve as California Senate Majority Leader, a post she held through 2008. She is the most legislatively experienced candidate in the lieutenant governor field. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician)) [(Ballotpedia — race)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026)

She switched her registration to Republican on September 4, 2024. [(California Globe — defection presser, Sept 2024)](https://californiaglobe.com/) [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician))

As of the June 2, 2026 top-two primary she is the California Republican Party–endorsed candidate and carries the highest GOP name recognition and experience in the field. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(Ballotpedia — race)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026)

**About the office she seeks:** The California Lieutenant Governor has limited independent power. The office's substantive roles are mostly ex-officio board seats — Regent of the University of California, Trustee of the California State University, and member (and chair) of the State Lands Commission — plus serving as President of the State Senate with a tie-breaking vote, and standing first in line of succession to the Governor. The UC Regent and CSU Trustee seats are the most direct levers for her education-reform agenda; most other "plans" below would depend on the Governor, Legislature, or other offices. (inference)

## Record / past performance

**Education reform (signature record):**
- Authored the Parent Empowerment Act, the "Parent Trigger" law (SBX5 4, 2010, with Sen. Bob Huff; signed by Gov. Schwarzenegger; passed by one vote in each house) — the first such law in the nation. It lets a majority of parents at a chronically failing school force restructuring; roughly 20 states later considered copies. [(EdWeek — "Why I Wrote a Parent-Trigger Law," 2013)](https://www.edweek.org/) [(NPR StateImpact)](https://stateimpact.npr.org/)
- Also authored the Open Enrollment Act. [(EdWeek)](https://www.edweek.org/)
- Led California Democrats for Education Reform (2010–2013) and founded the Foundation for Parent Empowerment / California Center for Parent Empowerment. [(California Policy Center)](https://californiapolicycenter.org/) [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician))
- Founder and Executive Director of Scholarship Prep Charter School (Santa Ana, TK–8, with a high-poverty / foster-youth focus). [(California Policy Center)](https://californiapolicycenter.org/)

**Legislative leadership:**
- First woman California Senate Majority Leader (2005–2008); chaired the Senate Education Committee. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician))

**Party switch and consistency:**
- Switched to Republican Sept 4, 2024, framed as principled realignment: "The Democratic Party has lost its moral compass with working class voters"; "I do not recognize it anymore... Adios!" [(California Globe — defection presser, Sept 2024)](https://californiaglobe.com/) [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician))
- Consistency in her favor: her education-reform and anti-union stance long predates the switch — she fought her own party as a Democrat on charters and the Parent Trigger from 2010 onward. (inference)
- Counter-signal: in the 2021 recall she endorsed Larry Elder while saying she "disagrees with just about everything Larry Elder stands for," an anti-Newsom tactical vote. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Romero_(politician)) [(California Globe)](https://californiaglobe.com/)

**Institutionalism / Trump / 2020:** She endorsed Trump in 2024 and is a registered Republican, framing the move as populist policy realignment rather than personalism — the GOP "has, under Donald Trump, become the champion of working people," and "I will vote for Donald Trump this fall." [(California Globe — defection presser, Sept 2024)](https://californiaglobe.com/) See Controversies / liabilities for what targeted searching did and did not find on 2020 / Jan. 6.

**2. "Golden Ticket" alliance — campaign branding, not a joint ticket:**
Romero has a voluntary campaign alliance with GOP gubernatorial candidate Steve Hilton, branded "California's Golden Ticket." To be clear, California elects its Governor and Lieutenant Governor **separately, not as a joint ticket** — the alliance is campaign branding and coordination, not a constitutional pairing. Voters cast independent votes for each office, and the two could in principle win or lose independently of each other. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/lieutenant-governor/) [(factually.co)](https://factually.co/)
