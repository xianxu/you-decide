# Rob Bonta — 2026 CA Attorney General candidate

## Background

**About the office:** The California Attorney General is the state's chief law officer and top prosecutor. The AG represents California in court, enforces state consumer-protection, environmental, civil-rights, and criminal law, oversees the California Department of Justice, and writes the official titles and summaries for statewide ballot measures. It is a statewide-executive, institutionalist-heavy office. ([CalMatters 2026 voter guide — Attorney General](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/attorney-general/) — secondary, nonpartisan)

Rob Bonta is the incumbent, seeking re-election to a full term. He is a former member of the California State Assembly representing the Oakland/Alameda area, was appointed Attorney General by Governor Gavin Newsom in 2021, and was elected to the office in 2022. He is the first Filipino American to serve as attorney general in any U.S. state. He declined to run for governor in 2026. ([Wikipedia — Rob Bonta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Bonta) — Tier C, orienting bio only)

Bonta is one of three candidates certified for the June 2, 2026 top-two primary; no other Democrat and only one Republican (Michael E. Gates) and one Green (Marjorie Mikels) filed. ([CA Secretary of State — certified candidate list](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf) — Tier A, official) No head-to-head polling for this race surfaced; as the sole Democratic incumbent in a deep-blue state facing only a minor-party Republican and a Green, he is the heavy favorite (inference from the certified field).

## Stated positions

**Institutionalism / litigation against the federal government:** Bonta's central message is defending California against federal-government actions. His office reports filing 37 lawsuits in roughly the first six months of the second Trump administration (leading or co-leading 23 of them) plus more than 40 amicus briefs. ([CA OAG — "Six Months into Second Trump Administration… Litigation Has Restored Over $168 Billion," 2025-08-04](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/six-months-second-trump-administration-attorney-general-bonta-reports-california) — Tier A, claim-by-the-office; self-reported)

**Immigration / sanctuary:** Robust defender of California's sanctuary law (the California Values Act / SB 54); his office has moved to dismiss challenges to the law, and the law has been upheld in court. ([CA OAG — "Bonta Urges Court to Dismiss Challenge to California Law Limiting Use of State and Local Resources for Immigration Enforcement"](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-urges-court-dismiss-challenge-california-law-limiting-use) — Tier A)

**Public safety / Proposition 36:** Proposition 36 (November 2024), which toughened theft, drug, and fentanyl penalties and rolled back parts of Prop 47, passed. Although Bonta has been aligned with the Prop 47 reform approach, on December 13, 2024 his office issued a law-enforcement information bulletin (2024-DLE-19) operationalizing Prop 36. ([CA OAG — "Bonta Releases Law Enforcement Bulletin on Proposition 36"](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-releases-law-enforcement-bulletin-proposition-36) — Tier A, self-reported)

**Homelessness / treatment-not-jail:** Bonta has warned against a "massive pendulum swing… [to] just a shift to punishment and criminalization" on homelessness and favored support services; his office sued to block federal HUD conditions seen as disadvantaging programs serving people with mental disabilities or substance-use disorders. ([Voice of San Diego — "AG Fears Homeless Enforcement 'Pendulum Swing,'" 2023-10-11](https://voiceofsandiego.org/2023/10/11/ag-fears-homeless-enforcement-pendulum-swing/) — secondary) ([CA OAG — "Bonta Files Lawsuit Against Trump Administration Over Funding Restrictions Expected to Worsen Homelessness Crisis"](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-files-lawsuit-against-trump-administration-over-funding) — Tier A)

**Police accountability:** Has vowed to release police-misconduct records and to investigate officer-involved shootings; co-authored AB 1506 (signed 2020), which requires the state DOJ to investigate police killings of unarmed civilians, and as AG stood up the implementation teams (CaPSIT). ([CA OAG — "Bonta Releases Full Guidance Package on AB 1506 Implementation"](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-releases-full-guidance-package-ab-1506-implementation) — Tier A)

**Death penalty:** Personally opposes the death penalty, though his office (as a matter of statutory duty) defends the existing death-penalty statute in court. ([Davis Vanguard — "California Supreme Court Orders Attorney General to Defend Death Penalty against Racial Bias Challenge," 2026-05](https://davisvanguard.org/2026/05/california-supreme-court-orders-attorney-general-to-defend-death-penalty-against-racial-bias-challenge/) — secondary) ([Death Penalty Information Center — petition challenging CA death penalty](https://deathpenaltyinfo.org/lawsuit-filed-at-the-california-supreme-court-alleges-racist-application-of-the-death-penalty-violates-the-state-constitution) — secondary)

## Record / past performance

**Litigation against the second Trump administration:** Bonta's office reports 37 lawsuits filed in roughly the first six months of the second Trump administration (leading/co-leading 23) plus 40+ amicus briefs. Suits target federal funding conditions and federalism questions. The office states it secured early relief in 17 of 19 cases where a district court ruled, and that its litigation "restored over $168 billion" to California (largely via a single early lawsuit challenging a federal funding freeze) plus an estimated $11.1 billion in protected grant funding. ([CA OAG — "Six Months into Second Trump Administration… Restored Over $168 Billion," 2025-08-04](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/six-months-second-trump-administration-attorney-general-bonta-reports-california) — Tier A; **self-reported by the office, not independently audited**)

**Rule-of-law / death-penalty marker (2026):** On May 27, 2026, in *Office of State Public Defender et al. v. Bonta*, the California Supreme Court ordered Bonta to "show cause" (in Sacramento County Superior Court) why the death penalty is not unconstitutional on racial-bias grounds, with a return due June 26, 2026. Bonta personally opposes the death penalty, yet his office defends the statute. ([Davis Vanguard — "California Supreme Court Orders Attorney General to Defend Death Penalty against Racial Bias Challenge," 2026-05](https://davisvanguard.org/2026/05/california-supreme-court-orders-attorney-general-to-defend-death-penalty-against-racial-bias-challenge/) — secondary) ([Death Penalty Information Center — petition challenging CA death penalty](https://deathpenaltyinfo.org/lawsuit-filed-at-the-california-supreme-court-alleges-racist-application-of-the-death-penalty-violates-the-state-constitution) — secondary)

**Proposition 36 implementation:** Despite his Prop 47-reform alignment, Bonta's office issued a December 13, 2024 information bulletin (2024-DLE-19) to operationalize the voter-approved Prop 36 (tougher theft/drug/fentanyl penalties). ([CA OAG — "Bonta Releases Law Enforcement Bulletin on Proposition 36"](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-releases-law-enforcement-bulletin-proposition-36) — Tier A, self-reported)

**Police accountability legislation:** As a legislator he co-authored AB 1506 (signed 2020), requiring the state DOJ to investigate police killings of unarmed civilians, and as AG released the implementation guidance package and stood up the investigation teams (CaPSIT). ([CA OAG — "Bonta Releases Full Guidance Package on AB 1506 Implementation"](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-releases-full-guidance-package-ab-1506-implementation) — Tier A)

## Controversies / liabilities

**"Behested payments" / nonprofit conflict optics:** A February 2021 CalMatters investigation reported that Bonta solicited "behested payments" from companies with business before the Legislature (including Google and PG&E; roughly $517,500 cited for 2014–2016) directed to a nonprofit (Literacy Lab) associated with his wife, Mia Bonta. Separately, his own Bonta California Progress Foundation — funded with roughly $75,000 from lobbying interests — gave $25,000 to Literacy Lab in 2018. Outside experts cited in the investigation said the arrangement **appears legal** but flagged conflict-of-interest optics. **No illegality is asserted here.** ([CalMatters investigation — political/legislative nonprofits, Bonta & wife](https://calmatters.org/projects/california-political-legislative-nonprofits-rob-bonta-wife/) — Tier B anchor; the article itself characterizes the arrangement as apparently legal)
