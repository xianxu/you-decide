# Marjorie Mikels — 2026 CA Attorney General candidate

## Background

**About the office:** The California Attorney General is the state's chief law officer and top prosecutor. The AG represents California in court, enforces state consumer-protection, environmental, civil-rights, and criminal law, oversees the California Department of Justice, and writes the official titles and summaries for statewide ballot measures. It is a statewide-executive, institutionalist-heavy office. ([CalMatters 2026 voter guide — Attorney General](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/attorney-general/) — secondary, nonpartisan)

Marjorie Mikels is a longtime Inland Empire (San Bernardino County) litigator who founded her own law firm in 1988. ([CalMatters 2026 voter guide — Attorney General](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/attorney-general/) — secondary) She runs as the Green Party candidate, endorsed as part of a "Left Unity Slate" jointly backed by the Green Party of California and the Peace and Freedom Party; her ballot designation is "Attorney/Justice Advocate." ([Left Unity Slate — Marjorie Mikels](https://leftunityslate.org/marjorie-mikels) — Tier A, the slate's own organizing page)

Mikels is one of three candidates certified for the June 2, 2026 top-two primary and is the only Green Party candidate who filed. ([CA Secretary of State — certified candidate list](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf) — Tier A, official) No head-to-head polling surfaced; as a Green-Party protest candidate against a sitting Democratic incumbent in a deep-blue state, she has no realistic path to the top two (inference from the certified field).
