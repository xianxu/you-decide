# Michael E. Gates — 2026 CA Attorney General candidate

## Background

**About the office:** The California Attorney General is the state's chief law officer and top prosecutor. The AG represents California in court, enforces state consumer-protection, environmental, civil-rights, and criminal law, oversees the California Department of Justice, and writes the official titles and summaries for statewide ballot measures. It is a statewide-executive, institutionalist-heavy office. ([CalMatters 2026 voter guide — Attorney General](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/attorney-general/) — secondary, nonpartisan)

Michael E. Gates is the Huntington Beach City Attorney, first elected in 2014 and re-elected in 2018 and 2022 (roughly ten years in the role). He is a former trial attorney. He announced on February 10, 2025 that he was joining the U.S. Department of Justice's Civil Rights Division as a Deputy Assistant Attorney General under the Trump administration; his resignation letter was dated November 8, 2025, after about ten months, and he returned to Huntington Beach. His ballot designation is "Huntington Beach City Attorney / former Deputy U.S. Attorney." ([LAist — "Huntington Beach city attorney joins the Trump administration Justice Department" (Feb 2025)](https://laist.com/brief/news/politics/huntington-beach-city-attorney-joins-the-trump-administration-justice-department) — secondary) ([LAist — "Michael Gates produces letter he says confirms he resigned from DOJ"](https://laist.com/news/huntington-beachs-michael-gates-produces-letter-he-says-confirms-he-resigned-from-doj) — secondary) ([Gates campaign — gates4ag.com](https://www.gates4ag.com/) — Tier A, candidate's own site)

Gates is one of three candidates certified for the June 2, 2026 top-two primary and is the only Republican who filed. ([CA Secretary of State — certified candidate list](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf) — Tier A, official) No head-to-head polling surfaced; as the only Republican against the Democratic incumbent, he is the presumptive #2 finisher / general-election opponent to incumbent Rob Bonta (inference from the certified field).
