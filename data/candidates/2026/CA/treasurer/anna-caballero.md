# Anna Caballero — 2026 CA Treasurer candidate

## Background

Anna Caballero is a Democrat and a California State Senator; her ballot designation in this race is "California State Senator." Her career runs from local to statewide office: Mayor of Salinas, then the California State Assembly, then the State Senate, with legislative work concentrated in agriculture and housing finance. Under Gov. Jerry Brown she served as Secretary of the Business, Consumer Services and Housing Agency, running a major state agency. CalMatters characterizes her as a "relative moderate" who has at times broken with the Legislature's progressive wing on environmental regulation and criminal justice. [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer) [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/)

She is widely framed as the second-place contender in the field and, if elected, would be the first Latina to hold statewide office in California. [(natlawreview)](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/senator-anna-caballero-could-make-california-history-first-latina-statewide)

**About the field:** This is an open seat — incumbent Treasurer Fiona Ma is termed out and running for Lieutenant Governor. The June 2, 2026 top-two primary draws a six-candidate field: Eleni Kounalakis (D), Anna Caballero (D), Tony Vazquez (D), Jennifer Hawks (R), David Serpa (R), and Glenn Turner (Green). Reporting widely frames the contest as a likely two-Democrat runoff between Kounalakis and Caballero, with Caballero the consensus second; there is no public head-to-head polling, so viability framing rests on reporter assessment and fundraising rather than survey data (inference). [(CalMatters race story)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/california-treasurer-election-2026/) [(KPBS)](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/05/in-the-six-way-race-to-be-californias-treasurer-it-likely-comes-down-to-two-democrats)

**About the office she seeks:** The California State Treasurer is the state's banker and its debt and investment manager — issuing and managing state bonds, running the roughly $200 billion-plus Pooled Money Investment Account, and administering savings programs including ScholarShare 529, CalABLE, and CalSavers. The Treasurer also sits on the boards of CalPERS and CalSTRS and chairs or serves on dozens of state finance and investment authorities. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer)

## Stated positions

- **Housing:** Use public-private partnerships to accelerate the building of affordable housing. [(LAist)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer) [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/)
- **Energy / industry:** Use the Treasurer's financing authority to back lower-carbon fuel industries, specifically hydrogen and dairy gas. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/)
- **Immigration / use of office ("ICE Out" plan):** A signature six-point plan to use the Treasurer's authority to block state-backed financing, investments, and bonds from supporting immigration enforcement or detention, and to require recipients of state-backed financing to comply with California's sanctuary laws. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/)
- **On the office itself:** Describes herself as not treating the Treasurer post as a "stepping stone." [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/)
