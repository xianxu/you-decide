# Jennifer Hawks — 2026 CA Treasurer candidate

## Background

Jennifer Hawks is the California Republican Party–endorsed candidate for State Treasurer, running with the ballot designation "Retired Businesswoman." She has been a Silicon Valley resident since 1983 and is now retired. Her professional background is in private-school administration and business operations: she served on the executive team at Sacred Heart Schools in Atherton. She is president of the Palo Alto Republican Women Federated. She has no prior elected-office experience, which she frames as a virtue — positioning herself as an independent outsider. [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer) [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/) [(Hawks campaign)](https://hawksforus.com/)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. The seat is open. The six-candidate field is widely framed as a likely two-Democrat runoff (Lieutenant Governor Eleni Kounalakis vs. State Senator Anna Caballero), which makes the Republicans (Hawks and David Serpa) and the Green (Glenn Turner) underdogs unless the two leading Democrats split the Democratic vote. [(CalMatters — California treasurer election 2026)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/california-treasurer-election-2026/)

**About the office she seeks:** The California State Treasurer is the state's banker and chief debt and investment manager. The office issues and manages the state's bonds, runs the Pooled Money Investment Account (a portfolio of roughly $200 billion or more in state and local funds), and administers savings programs including ScholarShare (the 529 college-savings plan), CalABLE, and CalSavers. The Treasurer also sits on the governing boards of the state's two large public-pension funds, CalPERS and CalSTRS. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer) Hawks's fiscal-watchdog platform (audits, reporting, financial discipline) maps onto the office's stewardship and transparency role, though the Treasurer operates within statutory and board structures rather than setting fiscal policy unilaterally.
