# David Serpa — 2026 CA Treasurer candidate

## Background

David Serpa is a Republican candidate for State Treasurer running with the ballot designation "Businessman/Author/Father." He is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and works as a real estate agent and businessman; he is also an author. He has run for office before: he was the Republican nominee in California's 39th Congressional District in 2024, losing the general election. [(Serpa campaign)](https://serpaforca.com/)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. The seat is open. The six-candidate field is widely framed as a likely two-Democrat runoff (Lieutenant Governor Eleni Kounalakis vs. State Senator Anna Caballero), which makes the Republicans (Serpa and party-endorsed Jennifer Hawks) and the Green (Glenn Turner) underdogs unless the two leading Democrats split the vote. [(CalMatters — California treasurer election 2026)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/california-treasurer-election-2026/)

**About the office he seeks:** The California State Treasurer is the state's banker and chief debt and investment manager. The office issues and manages the state's bonds, runs the Pooled Money Investment Account (a portfolio of roughly $200 billion or more in state and local funds), and administers savings programs including ScholarShare (the 529 college-savings plan), CalABLE, and CalSavers. The Treasurer also sits on the governing boards of the state's two large public-pension funds, CalPERS and CalSTRS. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer) Serpa's oversight-and-transparency platform maps onto the office's stewardship role, though the Treasurer works within statutory and board structures rather than setting tax or spending policy.
