# Glenn Turner — 2026 CA Treasurer candidate

## Background

Glenn Turner is the Green Party candidate for State Treasurer. He has no ballot designation in this race. He describes himself as a retired small-business owner with 30-plus years of business experience. [(SoS official candidate statement)](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/candidates/treasurer-candidate-statements.htm)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. The seat is open. The six-candidate field is widely framed as a likely two-Democrat runoff (Lieutenant Governor Eleni Kounalakis vs. State Senator Anna Caballero), which leaves the Green candidate a negligible underdog. [(CalMatters — California treasurer election 2026)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/california-treasurer-election-2026/)

**About the office he seeks:** The California State Treasurer is the state's banker and chief debt and investment manager. The office issues and manages the state's bonds, runs the Pooled Money Investment Account (a portfolio of roughly $200 billion or more in state and local funds), and administers savings programs including ScholarShare (the 529 college-savings plan), CalABLE, and CalSavers. The Treasurer also sits on the governing boards of the state's two large public-pension funds, CalPERS and CalSTRS. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer) Turner's platform is the most activist-investing in the field — but several of his marquee planks (a billionaire tax, student-debt jubilee, public banks) lie outside the Treasurer's unilateral authority; the levers closest to the office are the pension-fund board votes that bear on his divestment proposals.
