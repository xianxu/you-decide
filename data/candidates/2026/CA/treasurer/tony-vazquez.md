# Tony Vazquez — 2026 CA Treasurer candidate

## Background

Tony Vazquez is a Democrat and an elected member of the California State Board of Equalization; his ballot designation in this race is "Member, State Board of Equalization." A former schoolteacher and teacher trainer (degree from USC), he served on the Santa Monica City Council and was Santa Monica's first Latino mayor (2016) before being elected to the Board of Equalization's 3rd District (Los Angeles County) in November 2018 and re-elected in November 2022 — giving him tax-administration experience. He has also served as the Board's chair. [(BOE — Antonio Vazquez official bio)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/vazquez/about.htm) [(BOE — Vazquez elected BOE Chairman, 2023)](https://boe.ca.gov/news/2023/NR-23-02.htm)

He is the lowest-profile of the three Democrats in this race and is not part of the top-two conversation in reporting. [(CalMatters race story)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/california-treasurer-election-2026/)

**About the field:** This is an open seat — incumbent Treasurer Fiona Ma is termed out and running for Lieutenant Governor. The June 2, 2026 top-two primary draws a six-candidate field: Eleni Kounalakis (D), Anna Caballero (D), Tony Vazquez (D), Jennifer Hawks (R), David Serpa (R), and Glenn Turner (Green). Reporting widely frames the contest as a likely two-Democrat runoff between Kounalakis and Caballero; Vazquez is not in the top-two conversation, so his low-viability framing rests on reporter assessment rather than any public head-to-head polling (inference). [(CalMatters race story)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/california-treasurer-election-2026/) [(KPBS)](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/05/in-the-six-way-race-to-be-californias-treasurer-it-likely-comes-down-to-two-democrats)

**About the office he seeks:** The California State Treasurer is the state's banker and its debt and investment manager — issuing and managing state bonds, running the roughly $200 billion-plus Pooled Money Investment Account, and administering savings programs including ScholarShare 529, CalABLE, and CalSavers. The Treasurer also sits on the boards of CalPERS and CalSTRS and chairs or serves on dozens of state finance and investment authorities. [(CalMatters voter guide)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/treasurer/) [(LAist voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-treasurer)

## Stated positions

- **Housing:** Affordable housing is his stated priority; his campaign says he will "streamline permitting requirements and offer tax benefits to incentivize new housing development" and continue educational campaigns helping low-income applicants claim property-tax exemptions. [(Vazquez for Treasurer campaign)](https://vazquez4treasurer.com/)
