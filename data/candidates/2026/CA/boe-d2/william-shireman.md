# William Shireman — 2026 CA Board of Equalization D2

## Background

Bill Shireman is a San Francisco-based Republican, entrepreneur and environmental-business coalition-builder. UC Berkeley journalism graduate who taught leadership and negotiations at UC Berkeley's Haas Business School; President and Founder of the nonprofit Future 500, which brings business and environmental advocates together. His signature prior achievement: brokering California's "bottle bill" recycling deposit system (passed 1986, the campaign bio dates enactment to 1987) by uniting "an unlikely coalition in the form of Coors, Safeway and the Sierra Club" — a genuine cross-ideological coalition win. ([Bill Shireman for California — About](https://www.billshiremanforcalifornia.com/about-bill) — Tier A, candidate's official bio; re-fetched 2026-05-29) He is Vice Chair / an officer of the Lincoln Club of Northern California ([The Lincoln Club of Northern California](https://www.thelincolnclub.com/who_we_are) — Tier A, the organization's own roster). County GOP central committee member and former state GOP delegate per the regional GOP organ ([BayAreaGOP — profile](https://bayareagop.com/boe-candidate-bill-shireman-works-to-bring-opponents-together/) — Tier C, party-affiliated/candidate-friendly; party-internal roles only). Lost the 2022 CA Assembly District 17 Republican primary to incumbent Matt Haney and David Campos. ([KRON4 — "Meet the Republican trying to woo SF voters"](https://www.kron4.com/news/bay-area/meet-the-republican-trying-to-woo-sf-voters/) — Tier B)

## Stated positions

Ballot designation "Taxpayer Advocate"; self-described "fiscal conservative with a conscience." ([CalMatters — BOE voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/board-of-equalization/) — Tier B) Per his official candidate statement, the BOE platform is: ([CA SoS Voter Information Guide — BOE candidate statements](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/candidates/boe-candidate-statements.htm) — Tier A)

1. Ensuring taxes are administered competently and transparently
2. Protecting Proposition 13 while modernizing it
3. Defending taxpayers from unfair assessments
4. Promoting housing affordability and opportunity as a tax-reform goal
5. Credits "a self-funded recycling program" that "recycled over half a trillion cans, plastics, computers and more, with zero reliance on tax dollars"

He has separately framed the office's value in terms of its "soft power" — "extraordinary power that I intend to tap" — to pursue broader tax reform beyond the BOE's post-2017 narrow mandate. ([Indivisible Ventura — BOE D2 analysis](https://indivisibleventura.org/state-board-of-equalization-member-district-2-2026-primary-election/) — Tier B, hostile/progressive source, quoting his questionnaire)
