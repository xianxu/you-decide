# J. Brett Marymee — 2026 CA Board of Equalization, District 2 candidate

## Background

J. Brett Marymee is a Republican candidate for the California State Board of Equalization, District 2, in the June 2, 2026 top-two primary. He is a Santa Ynez (Santa Barbara County) resident and a long-serving local special-district official, running on a fiscal-conservative, anti-tax platform with broad Santa Barbara County GOP and statewide conservative-group backing.

**Profession / career:** Marymee describes himself as an aerospace and defense multi-discipline engineering professional with roughly 40 years (his campaign bio says "over five decades") of experience, having contributed to projects including the B-2a Stealth Bomber, the B-1b Bomber, and the Missile Defense Ground-based Interceptor. He is also a small business owner (he states 32 years as such). ([jbrettmarymee.com — About](https://www.jbrettmarymee.com/about) — Tier A, candidate's official bio; [VOTE411 — BOE D2 candidate detail](https://onyourballot.vote411.org/m/candidate-detail.do?id=72193447) — Tier B, nonpartisan voter guide quoting his questionnaire)

**Public office held:** Elected Director of the **Santa Ynez River Water Conservation District (SYRWCD)**, Division 5 (representing Santa Ynez, Solvang, East Buellton, Ballard and Los Olivos), serving since November 4, 2014; currently Vice-President, with his current term ending December 4, 2026. **About the office:** The Board of Equalization is California's only elected tax board. Before 2017 it was a powerful agency that collected roughly a third of state tax revenue and adjudicated taxpayer appeals before elected members. The 2017 Taxpayer Transparency and Fairness Act stripped most of that authority: sales/use and special-tax administration moved to the new California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA), and tax appeals moved to the Office of Tax Appeals, leaving the BOE only its original 1879 constitutional duties — overseeing **property-tax assessment uniformity across counties**, **assessing state-assessed property (utilities, railroads, pipelines that cross county lines)**, and administering the **alcoholic-beverage tax** and **insurance tax**. ([CalMatters — BOE election](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/board-of-equalization-election/) — Tier B; [CalMatters — BOE voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/board-of-equalization/) — Tier B) Board members are paid about **$184,000/year**, and critics describe the elected seats as a "sinecure for retired legislators," though a retired county assessor (Larry Stone) credited the professional staff that "really drives it." ([The Almanac — "Should the state's tax commission even exist?" (2026-05)](https://www.almanacnews.com/election/2026/05/14/should-the-states-tax-commission-even-exist/) — Tier B) **District 2** is a large, Democratic-leaning coastal district stretching from Del Norte County on the Oregon border south to Ventura County (~19 counties, ~10.5 million residents). ([CalMatters — BOE voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/board-of-equalization/) — Tier B; district scale per [The Almanac](https://www.almanacnews.com/election/2026/05/14/should-the-states-tax-commission-even-exist/) — Tier B) The full **field of six**: Sally J. Lieber (D, incumbent), John Pimentel (D), and Republicans J. Brett Marymee, Mark McComas, Bill Shireman, and John W. Zaruka. ([CalMatters — BOE voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/board-of-equalization/) — Tier B)
