# Patrick Wolff — 2026 CA Insurance Commissioner candidate

## Background

Patrick Wolff is a Democrat with a finance background and an unusual résumé: a Harvard graduate (magna cum laude), a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA), and a two-time U.S. Chess Champion and chess grandmaster. His ballot designation in this race is "Financial Analyst." He reports 25-plus years in business and finance, including building a home- and auto-insurance brokerage at a major bank and analyzing insurers (among them Berkshire Hathaway); he obtained his property-and-casualty (P&C) license around the time he decided to run. Per his campaign, the brokerage he built was at **Capital One**, and he spent four years focused on the insurance space. [(Patrick Wolff campaign site)](https://patrickwolff.com/) [(SoS — official Insurance Commissioner candidate statement)](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/candidates/insurance-commissioner-candidate-statements.htm) [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/)

**About the field:** This is an open seat — incumbent Insurance Commissioner Ricardo Lara (D) is term-limited, having been elected in 2018 and re-elected in 2022, and is not on the ballot. The June 2, 2026 top-two primary draws an 11-candidate field. The serious field is four Democrats — Ben Allen, Steven Bradford, Jane Kim, and Patrick Wolff — plus two Republicans, Korsgaden and Farren. Wolff has low name identification, and his viability is uncertain and likely lower than the other Democrats (inference). California history cuts against a two-Democrat runoff: a Republican has advanced in 8 of the last 9 commissioner races since 1988, so the likeliest top-two is one Democrat plus the GOP-endorsed Korsgaden (inference). [(Ballotpedia — 2026 Insurance Commissioner election)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_Insurance_Commissioner_election,_2026_(June_2_top-two_primary)) [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/)

**About the office he seeks:** The California Insurance Commissioner is the elected regulator of the largest property-insurance market in the United States. The office approves or denies insurer rate-change requests, runs the Department of Insurance, and administers both Proposition 103 (the 1988 voter initiative governing rate regulation) and the FAIR Plan (the state's insurer of last resort). The job centers on balancing consumer protection against keeping insurers writing policies in California amid a wildfire-driven availability crisis. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Insurance Commissioner voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-insurance-commissioner)

Wolff runs as the most explicitly anti-mandate, pro-incentive Democrat; technocratic and cost/availability/adaptation-oriented, and he avoids climate-catastrophe rhetoric.

That's not true." — favors creating conditions and aligning incentives rather than mandating outcomes.
