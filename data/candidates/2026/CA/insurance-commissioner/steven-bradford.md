# Steven Bradford — 2026 CA Insurance Commissioner candidate

## Background

Steven Bradford is a Democrat and a former California State Senator (SD-35) and former Assemblymember, with a long legislative career that ran from the Gardena City Council to the Assembly to the State Senate; he left office in December 2024. His ballot designation in this race is "Education Organization Boardmember." In the Senate he served on the Senate Insurance Committee, giving him direct prior exposure to insurance policy. [(Capitol Weekly — "Meet the Insurance Commissioner candidates: Steven Bradford")](https://capitolweekly.net/meet-the-insurance-commissioner-candidates-steven-bradfor/) [(Carrier Management — candidate feature, May 15 2026)](https://www.carriermanagement.com/features/2026/05/15/287715.htm)

**About the field:** This is an open seat — incumbent Insurance Commissioner Ricardo Lara (D) is term-limited, having been elected in 2018 and re-elected in 2022, and is not on the ballot. The June 2, 2026 top-two primary draws an 11-candidate field. The serious field is four Democrats — Ben Allen, Steven Bradford, Jane Kim, and Patrick Wolff — plus two Republicans, Korsgaden and Farren. California history cuts against a two-Democrat runoff: a Republican has advanced in 8 of the last 9 commissioner races since 1988, so the likeliest top-two is one Democrat plus the GOP-endorsed Korsgaden (inference). [(Ballotpedia — 2026 Insurance Commissioner election)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_Insurance_Commissioner_election,_2026_(June_2_top-two_primary)) [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/)

**About the office he seeks:** The California Insurance Commissioner is the elected regulator of the largest property-insurance market in the United States. The office approves or denies insurer rate-change requests, runs the Department of Insurance, and administers both Proposition 103 (the 1988 voter initiative governing rate regulation) and the FAIR Plan (the state's insurer of last resort). The job centers on balancing consumer protection against keeping insurers writing policies in California amid a wildfire-driven availability crisis. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Insurance Commissioner voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-insurance-commissioner)
