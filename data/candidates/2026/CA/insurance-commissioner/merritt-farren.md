# Merritt Farren — 2026 CA Insurance Commissioner candidate

## Background

Merritt Farren is a Republican running for California Insurance Commissioner; he presents as a consumer advocate and attorney. He holds degrees from Stanford and UC Berkeley Law and spent 30-plus years as a technology executive at Disney and Amazon. He lost his home in the January 2025 Palisades fire and subsequently intervened formally as a consumer advocate in the State Farm rate proceeding before the Department of Insurance — giving him hands-on experience with the CDI rate process plus a personal stake in the wildfire-insurance crisis. [(CalMatters — commentary: Palisades fire and the insurance commissioner race)](https://calmatters.org/commentary/2026/04/palisades-fire-insurance-commissioner-race/) [(CA Secretary of State — certified candidate list)](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. The seat is open: incumbent Ricardo Lara is term-limited. The field has 11 candidates, and the top two advance. Farren does not have the California Republican Party endorsement (Korsgaden does), and as a second Republican he is likely to split the GOP vote, lowering his viability for a top-two slot. **About the office he seeks:** The California Insurance Commissioner is an elected statewide regulator who runs the Department of Insurance and oversees the largest property-insurance market in the United States. The Commissioner approves or denies insurers' rate filings, enforces Proposition 103 (the 1988 measure that made the office elective and requires prior approval of rate changes), oversees the FAIR Plan (the insurer-of-last-resort pool), and must balance consumer protection against keeping insurers willing to write policies in California amid the wildfire-driven availability and affordability crisis. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Insurance Commissioner voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-insurance-commissioner)

## Stated positions

Farren offers the most technically detailed market solution in the field; the planks below draw on his SPUR forum remarks and CalMatters coverage.

- **"CAL Reinsure" — a state reinsurance authority:** His central proposal is a state reinsurance authority that isolates catastrophic, community-level wildfire risk and moves it off individual insurers' books — modeled on Florida's Hurricane Catastrophe Fund and the UK's Flood Re. It would be funded by insurer fees, with municipal-status bonds available if reserves fall short. The goal is to make insurers willing to write standard policies statewide again and to shrink reliance on the FAIR Plan. [(SPUR — rebuilding an affordable, sustainable insurance market)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner) [(CalMatters — California insurance candidates and public options)](https://calmatters.org/economy/2026/05/california-insurance-candidates-public-options/)
- **30-day total-loss payout mandate:** Require full payout within 30 days on total losses. [(SPUR — rebuilding an affordable, sustainable insurance market)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **CDI modernization + community standards:** Tech-driven modernization of the Department of Insurance, and minimum community wildfire-protection standards. He argues good leadership could accomplish much "without the legislature at all." [(SPUR — rebuilding an affordable, sustainable insurance market)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner) [(CalMatters — California insurance candidates and public options)](https://calmatters.org/economy/2026/05/california-insurance-candidates-public-options/)
- **Climate framing (pragmatic, availability-and-cost):** His framing centers on restoring availability and affordability of coverage rather than a climate-catastrophe argument. (inference, from the emphasis of his proposals.)
