# Stacy Korsgaden — 2026 CA Insurance Commissioner candidate

## Background

Stacy Korsgaden is a Republican running for California Insurance Commissioner; her ballot designation is "Licensed Insurance Agent." She is a San Luis Obispo–area insurance-agency owner with roughly 40 years in the business, selling and advising on home, auto, life, health, and business insurance plus financial advising — the most directly operational insurance-industry experience in the field. She previously ran for San Luis Obispo County Supervisor in 2020 and 2022 and lost both times. [(Capitol Weekly — insurance commissioner candidates)](https://capitolweekly.net/21160-2/) [(Ballotpedia — Stacy Korsgaden)](https://ballotpedia.org/Stacy_Korsgaden)

This is the June 2, 2026 top-two primary, with the general on November 3, 2026. The seat is open: incumbent Ricardo Lara is term-limited. The field has 11 candidates, and the top two advance. Historically a Republican has advanced to the general in 8 of the 9 commissioner races since the office became elected in 1988; as the California Republican Party–endorsed candidate, Korsgaden is the likely Republican top-two contender. (inference, from the CAGOP endorsement and the historical pattern.) [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/)

**About the office she seeks:** The California Insurance Commissioner is an elected statewide regulator who runs the Department of Insurance and oversees the largest property-insurance market in the United States. The Commissioner approves or denies insurers' rate filings, enforces Proposition 103 (the 1988 measure that made the office elective and requires prior approval of rate changes), oversees the FAIR Plan (the insurer-of-last-resort pool), and must balance consumer protection against keeping insurers willing to write policies in California amid the wildfire-driven availability and affordability crisis. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Insurance Commissioner voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-california-insurance-commissioner)

## Stated positions

Korsgaden runs the most explicitly pro-market platform in the field; the planks below draw on her campaign's published plan.

- **"New Business Division" to recruit carriers:** Within roughly her first 100 days she would establish a New Business Division inside the Department of Insurance to recruit and expedite admitted carriers into California, with the goal of relieving strain on the FAIR Plan and on legacy carriers and restoring competition. [(Korsgaden campaign — My Plan)](https://www.stacyforinsurancecommissioner.com/myplan)
- **Restore competition / lower costs:** She frames California's problem as market dysfunction — too few carriers competing — and proposes lowering costs through fraud reduction and product innovation, aiming for a "free and fair market that actually works for consumers." [(Korsgaden campaign — My Plan)](https://www.stacyforinsurancecommissioner.com/myplan)
- **Climate framing (pragmatic, cost-and-availability):** Her diagnosis emphasizes market dysfunction, fraud, and lack of competition rather than a climate-catastrophe frame. (inference, from the emphasis of her published plan.) [(Korsgaden campaign — My Plan)](https://www.stacyforinsurancecommissioner.com/myplan)
