# Jane Kim — 2026 CA Insurance Commissioner candidate

## Background

Jane Kim is a Democrat, a civil-rights attorney, and a former two-term San Francisco Supervisor who now serves as the California director of the Working Families Party; she was also a Bernie Sanders 2020 organizer. Her background is political and advocacy-based rather than insurance-technical. [(SF Standard — Jane Kim profile)](https://sfstandard.com/2026/04/20/jane-kim-insurance-commissioner/) [(American Prospect — single-payer disaster insurance)](https://prospect.org/2026/05/14/jane-kim-candidate-pitching-single-payer-disaster-insurance-california/)

**About the field:** This is an open seat — incumbent Insurance Commissioner Ricardo Lara (D) is term-limited, having been elected in 2018 and re-elected in 2022, and is not on the ballot. The June 2, 2026 top-two primary draws an 11-candidate field. The serious field is four Democrats — Ben Allen, Steven Bradford, Jane Kim, and Patrick Wolff — plus two Republicans, Korsgaden and Farren. Kim is competitive for a Democratic top-two slot and is described as ideologically polarizing. California history cuts against a two-Democrat runoff: a Republican has advanced in 8 of the last 9 commissioner races since 1988, so the likeliest top-two is one Democrat plus the GOP-endorsed Korsgaden (inference). [(Ballotpedia — 2026 Insurance Commissioner election)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_Insurance_Commissioner_election,_2026_(June_2_top-two_primary)) [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/)

**About the office she seeks:** The California Insurance Commissioner is the elected regulator of the largest property-insurance market in the United States. The office approves or denies insurer rate-change requests, runs the Department of Insurance, and administers both Proposition 103 (the 1988 voter initiative governing rate regulation) and the FAIR Plan (the state's insurer of last resort). The job centers on balancing consumer protection against keeping insurers writing policies in California amid a wildfire-driven availability crisis. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Insurance Commissioner voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-insurance-commissioner)

## Stated positions

Kim runs on a populist, structural-overhaul platform.

- **"Disaster Insurance for All" (flagship):** A state-run nonprofit, single-payer-style catastrophe pool funded by a slice of premiums, modeled on the systems in France and New Zealand. (This proposal would require legislation beyond the powers of the office itself.) [(American Prospect — single-payer disaster insurance)](https://prospect.org/2026/05/14/jane-kim-candidate-pitching-single-payer-disaster-insurance-california/) [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **FAIR Plan board:** Restructure the FAIR Plan board to add consumer advocates. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Statewide writing rules:** Rules forcing insurers to write statewide rather than cherry-pick lower-risk areas. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Rate freeze after a claim:** Freeze rates after a claim is filed. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Payment-delay penalties:** Penalize insurer payment delays. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Non-renewal notice:** Extend the non-renewal notice period to six months. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Transparency:** Public insurer "report cards." [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Rejects competition as the fix:** Explicitly rejects more competition as a solution — "people don't actually lower their prices just because there's more competition... corporations don't look out for you." [(CalMatters — insurance candidates on public options)](https://calmatters.org/economy/2026/05/california-insurance-candidates-public-options/)
