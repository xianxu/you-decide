# Ben Allen — 2026 CA Insurance Commissioner candidate

## Background

Ben Allen is a Democrat and a third-term California State Senator representing Senate District 24, which includes the Pacific Palisades — the burn zone of the January 2025 Palisades fire. His ballot designation in this race is "California State Senator." In the Legislature he has authored fire-victim relief bills (including tax relief, simplified personal-property loss claims, and penalties tied to claim deadlines) and led the campaign for Proposition 4, the $10 billion climate-resilience bond. [(Carrier Management — candidate feature, May 15 2026)](https://www.carriermanagement.com/features/2026/05/15/287397.htm) [(Press Democrat editorial)](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2026/05/22/pd-editorial-ben-allen-is-best-pick-for-insurance-post/)

**About the field:** This is an open seat — incumbent Insurance Commissioner Ricardo Lara (D) is term-limited, having been elected in 2018 and re-elected in 2022, and is not on the ballot. The June 2, 2026 top-two primary draws an 11-candidate field. The serious field is four Democrats — Ben Allen, Steven Bradford, Jane Kim, and Patrick Wolff — plus two Republicans, Korsgaden and Farren. Allen is widely framed as the Democratic front-runner. California history cuts against a two-Democrat runoff: a Republican has advanced in 8 of the last 9 commissioner races since 1988, so the likeliest top-two is one Democrat plus the GOP-endorsed Korsgaden (inference). [(Ballotpedia — 2026 Insurance Commissioner election)](https://ballotpedia.org/California_Insurance_Commissioner_election,_2026_(June_2_top-two_primary)) [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/)

**About the office he seeks:** The California Insurance Commissioner is the elected regulator of the largest property-insurance market in the United States. The office approves or denies insurer rate-change requests, runs the Department of Insurance, and administers both Proposition 103 (the 1988 voter initiative governing rate regulation) and the FAIR Plan (the state's insurer of last resort). The job centers on balancing consumer protection against keeping insurers writing policies in California amid a wildfire-driven availability crisis. [(CalMatters — 2026 voter guide, Insurance Commissioner)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/insurance-commissioner/) [(LAist — 2026 CA primary, Insurance Commissioner voter guide)](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-insurance-commissioner)

## Stated positions

Allen presents as pro-market operating within Proposition 103.

- **Faster rate review:** Resolve rate filings "in months instead of years." [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Catastrophe modeling:** Supports "responsible use of forward-looking catastrophe models with transparency and oversight so rates better reflect risk," broadly backing the direction of the Department's Sustainable Insurance Strategy. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Department modernization:** CDI staffing increases and digital modernization. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Home hardening:** Home-hardening incentives plus low-interest loans. [(SPUR forum coverage)](https://www.spur.org/news/2026-05-19/rebuilding-affordable-sustainable-insurance-market-insurance-commissioner)
- **Reinsurance backstop:** Open to a state reinsurance backstop, framed "like FDIC." [(CalMatters — insurance candidates on public options)](https://calmatters.org/economy/2026/05/california-insurance-candidates-public-options/)
- **Climate / cost-sharing:** The most climate-forward of the front-runners; argues the fossil-fuel industry "has a role to play" in costs and that you "can't have the public bear all the cost." [(CalMatters — insurance candidates on public options)](https://calmatters.org/economy/2026/05/california-insurance-candidates-public-options/)
