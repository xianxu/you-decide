These are attributed priorities; this review has not established how he would fund each commitment or vote on every disputed district policy. [Pulse profile](https://www.rwcpulse.com/election/2026/08/28/sequoia-districts-youngest-incumbent-drops-out-of-board-race-endorses-opponent/)

**Sathvik Nori:** Elected in 2022 soon after graduating from Menlo-Atherton. His withdrawal statement credits shared district work and describes Dekshenieks as a committed student advocate. It should not be recast as a current competing platform. [Pulse reporting](https://www.rwcpulse.com/election/2026/08/28/sequoia-districts-youngest-incumbent-drops-out-of-board-race-endorses-opponent/)

The Daily Journal reported strong family opposition; Nori acknowledged other budget options but supported the final decision and pledged help for affected families. This establishes a contested vote, not misconduct. [Daily Journal](https://www.smdailyjournal.com/news/local/tide-academy-will-close/article_ee29479b-52b6-4240-a433-47284a3bf005.html)
