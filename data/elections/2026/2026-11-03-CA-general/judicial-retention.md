## What the votes mean

Each justice receives an independent, nonpartisan Yes/No retention vote. Yes approves continued service; No rejects retention. Appointments require gubernatorial nomination and Commission on Judicial Appointments confirmation before voter confirmation. Full terms are 12 years; initial retention can cover a predecessor's remaining term. These ballot questions need not confer identical terms. [Secretary of State explanation](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/justices/supreme-court-justices/).
