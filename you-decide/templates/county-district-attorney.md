# Office template — County District Attorney

County-level chief prosecutor. Sets charging priorities, controls plea bargaining, runs felony prosecutions, oversees gang/drug/sex-crime/financial-crime units. The DA's discretion is enormous — what to charge, what to drop, what to plead — and is essentially unreviewable by other elected officials. **Institutionalist axis dominates** because procedural justice and prosecutorial restraint are the office's core stewardship.

## Weights

Scale: heavy (×2), medium (×1), light (×0.5).

### Heavy (×2)
- **Institutionalist** — prosecutorial restraint, charging discretion exercised within precedent, willingness to drop weak cases, resistance to political pressure on charging decisions. Brady compliance. Independence from sheriff and police chiefs.
- **Public-safety-strict-but-care** — strict on violent / repeat / sex / financial crimes; humane on first-offense / mental-health / addiction / homelessness. The mentally-ill-care-not-jail axis is very salient (DA decides whether to divert to treatment).
- **Smart/logically-consistent** — legal craft matters; DA's job demands case-by-case judgment + clear reasoning publicly defensible
- **Anti-hypocrisy** — public-trust role; DA scandals tend to be career-ending

### Medium (×1)
- **Anti-personalist-strongman** — DA who builds personal brand vs. office craft is a tell
- **Anti-tax-spend** — DA office budget; tone on bond measures and asset-forfeiture revenue
- **Immigration** — county-level cooperation/non-cooperation with federal ICE (post-conviction notification, ICE-hold compliance)

### Light (×0.5)
- All other policy axes (housing, energy, education, healthcare, crypto) — no policy lever
