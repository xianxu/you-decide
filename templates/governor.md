# Office template — Governor

Axis weights for state-governor races. Governor is the highest-leverage state-level executive: controls budget proposals, signs/vetoes legislation, appoints judges, sets enforcement priorities. Most Tier 4 issue axes are directly under the governor's lever.

## Weights

Scale: **heavy** (×2), **medium** (×1), **light** (×0.5). Hard filters are still hard filters regardless of weight.

### Heavy (×2)
- **Housing — zoning-realism** — CA housing crisis is the state's single most-felt failure; governor signs CEQA/zoning reform.
- **Energy — cost-down-not-just-green** — CA energy prices are highest in continental US; governor sets ratepayer and grid policy.
- **Public safety — strict + mentally-ill-care** — governor sets prosecution priorities, prison/jail policy, mental-health funding.
- **Builder-mentality** — infrastructure pace is bottlenecked by governor's regulatory posture.
- **Anti-tax-and-spend / fiscal-conservative** — governor's budget shapes the cause-bond pipeline.
- **Institutionalist** — governor handles election integrity (Secretary of State works under), pardons, emergency powers.

### Medium (×1)
- **Immigration** — sanctuary-state posture, federal cooperation, state law enforcement coordination.
- **Healthcare** — Medi-Cal, drug pricing, hospital regulation.
- **Education-investment** — governor proposes K-12 / UC / CC budgets; influence is real but bounded by initiatives.
- **Character / smart-and-logically-consistent** — universal Tier 1.
- **Anti-hypocrisy** — universal Tier 1.

### Light (×0.5)
- **Crypto** — minimal state-level lever.
- **Anti-personalist-strongman / Trump-character** — relevant for character read but governor doesn't directly enable national personalist mode.
- **Socially-liberal / leave-people-alone** — mostly federal/judicial layer; governor influences via regulation but not primary lever.
- **Secular-pluralist** — universal but rarely the decisive axis at gov level in CA.
- **Anti-union, pro-ownership-floor** — governor influences via budget/labor-relations posture but isn't the primary lever.
- **Work + opportunity belief** — values lens applied to all economic axes; not scored separately at this office.

## Notes from first population (2026 CA governor)

- The agent-produced candidate profiles over-weighted **anti-hypocrisy** in their initial alignment reads because the other Tier 4 axes weren't first-class in `philosophy-xian.md` at the time. Re-applying with this template gives a meaningfully different ranking — Villaraigosa and Mahan jump on housing/energy/builder/fiscal alignment despite hypocrisy hits; Steyer collapses on builder + anti-tax-and-spend; Becerra's institutionalist credential is heavy at this office.
- Hard filters were initially set to (2020-fraud, ballot-seizure), which would have eliminated the entire viable GOP field in CA-2026 (Hilton and Bianco). Demoted 2026-05-27 to strong-negative scoring under [[calibration-skills/trump-era-cater-discount]]. With discount applied, both candidates re-enter the matrix; institutional concerns substantially discount their totals but no longer auto-reject. The action-vs-rhetoric distinction (Bianco's executive action vs. Hilton's commentary) is the open meta-question of the discount framework.
- **CEQA reform stance** emerged as a strong proxy for housing-zoning-realism + builder-mentality simultaneously. Candidates with explicit CEQA-reform records score on both axes from the same fact.
- **"All-of-the-above" energy positioning** is a strong positive proxy for energy-cost-not-just-green; candidates who use this phrase tend to align with Xian's energy axis.
