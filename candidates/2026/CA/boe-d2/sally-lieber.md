# Sally Lieber — 2026 CA Board of Equalization D2

## Background

Sally J. Lieber (born ~1960s, Mountain View area) is the incumbent BOE District 2 member, representing 19 coastal counties from Del Norte to Ventura (~10 million residents). She was elected in November 2022, defeating Republican Peter Verbica, and took office January 2023. She served as BOE Chair in 2024 and 2026, Vice Chair in 2025.

Prior political career:
- Mountain View City Council (1998–2002), Vice Mayor, Mayor
- Chair, Santa Clara Valley Water District commission
- Board member, Valley Transportation Authority
- CA State Assembly, 22nd District (2002–2008, three terms); Speaker pro Tempore in 2007–2008 session — the third woman to hold that office
- Term-limited out of Assembly 2008
- Lost CA State Senate primary 2010
- Lost Santa Clara County Board of Supervisors District 5 general election November 2024 (ran simultaneously with BOE service)

[(BOE official bio)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/lieber/about.htm) [(Ballotpedia)](https://ballotpedia.org/Sally_Lieber)

## Stated positions

From her GrowSF questionnaire and BOE official page [(GrowSF questionnaire)](https://growsf.org/voter-guide/questionnaires/june-2026/sally-lieber/) [(BOE page)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/lieber/):

- **BOE mandate**: Supports the post-2017 narrowed BOE scope. Describes board as "the right size in the system right now." Opposes technology overreach; prefers working with professional associations.
- **Fairness**: Wants auditing of assessor practices for consistency; advocates "equity between commercial and residential" rights under Prop 13.
- **Affordable housing**: Aims to streamline tax credit access for affordable housing developers; ensure families receive CalEITC and Young Child Tax Credits.
- **Transparency**: Prioritizes education on Prop 19 and other voter-approved initiatives.
- **Small business**: Technical assistance to small business owners on tax matters.
- **Disaster relief**: Ensure Prop 8 disaster assistance is accessible following fires, floods.

Self-described priorities: transparency/accountability, affordable housing, environmental protection, veterans' and homeowners' services.
