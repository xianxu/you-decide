# Sally Lieber — 2026 CA Board of Equalization D2

## Background

Sally J. Lieber (born ~1960s, Mountain View area) is the incumbent BOE District 2 member, representing 19 coastal counties from Del Norte to Ventura (~10 million residents). She was elected in November 2022, defeating Republican Peter Verbica, and took office January 2023. She served as BOE Chair in 2024 and 2026, Vice Chair in 2025.

Prior political career:
- Mountain View City Council (1998–2002), Vice Mayor, Mayor
- Chair, Santa Clara Valley Water District commission
- Board member, Valley Transportation Authority
- CA State Assembly, 22nd District (2002–2008, three terms); Speaker pro Tempore in 2007–2008 session — the third woman to hold that office
- Term-limited out of Assembly 2008
- Lost CA State Senate primary 2010
- Lost Santa Clara County Board of Supervisors District 5 general election November 2024 (ran simultaneously with BOE service)

[(BOE official bio)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/lieber/about.htm) [(Ballotpedia)](https://ballotpedia.org/Sally_Lieber)

## Stated positions

From her GrowSF questionnaire and BOE official page [(GrowSF questionnaire)](https://growsf.org/voter-guide/questionnaires/june-2026/sally-lieber/) [(BOE page)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/lieber/):

- **BOE mandate**: Supports the post-2017 narrowed BOE scope. Describes board as "the right size in the system right now." Opposes technology overreach; prefers working with professional associations.
- **Fairness**: Wants auditing of assessor practices for consistency; advocates "equity between commercial and residential" rights under Prop 13.
- **Affordable housing**: Aims to streamline tax credit access for affordable housing developers; ensure families receive CalEITC and Young Child Tax Credits.
- **Transparency**: Prioritizes education on Prop 19 and other voter-approved initiatives.
- **Small business**: Technical assistance to small business owners on tax matters.
- **Disaster relief**: Ensure Prop 8 disaster assistance is accessible following fires, floods.

Self-described priorities: transparency/accountability, affordable housing, environmental protection, veterans' and homeowners' services.

## Record / past performance

**BOE 2023–present:**
- Served as Chair 2024, Vice Chair 2025, Chair 2026. [(BOE news)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/lieber/news.htm)
- December 2024: Convened a special FTB hearing titled "Work, Assets, and the Safety Net," framed around income inequality and essential workers. Witness lineup included labor unions, poverty advocates, housing/budget organizations. Stated: "We have a moral obligation to address the needs of struggling Californians." This exceeds the BOE's narrow post-2017 mandate (property-tax administration, alcohol/tobacco/fuel tax, appeals); it is the clearest documented instance of activist use of board platform. [(BOE press release)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/lieber/24-01.htm)
- No documented tax-ruling controversies or administrative misconduct in the public record through May 2026.
- 2025: Submitted memo on AB 53 (Ramos) regarding legislative position. [(BOE memo)](https://boe.ca.gov/meetings/pdf/2025/202502-MI-Leg-AB53-Ramos.pdf)

**CA Assembly 2002–2008:**
- Key bills authored: CA Trafficking Victims Protection Act (AB 22, trafficking a felony); Sexual Assault Victim's Bill of Rights; expanded Assault Weapons Ban; hate crime "panic defense" ban; women inmate protections; Bay Restoration Authority (created San Francisco Bay restoration levy); minimum wage increases; expanded Cal Grants; supportive housing expansion.
- Speaker pro Tempore 2007–2008.
- Revenue and Taxation Committee: three terms, relevant background for BOE role.
- Controversial 2004 emissions bill (AB — vehicle smog exemption fixed at 1976 cutoff, overriding prior rolling 30-year exemption). Criticized by car collectors including Jay Leno as imposing severe penalties for negligible environmental benefit. Signed by Schwarzenegger. [(Ballotpedia)](https://ballotpedia.org/Sally_Lieber)
- 2007: Announced legislation to ban spanking of children under 3. Bill attracted intense national media attention and criticism for government overreach; the bill was ultimately dropped.

**Mountain View local:**
- Implemented local tobacco control legislation, green energy initiatives, affordable housing efforts. [(BOE about page)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/lieber/about.htm)

## Controversies / liabilities

1. **2007 spanking ban bill**: Proposed criminalizing spanking of under-3s — widely criticized as government overreach into family/parenting. Bill was dropped before a vote. Aged 19 years; qualifies for aged-flaws discount but signals a nanny-state interventionist instinct.

2. **2004 emissions exemption cutoff**: Changed rolling 30-year smog exemption to a fixed 1976 cutoff, affecting classic car owners with negligible environmental benefit. Aged 22 years; not a character flaw per se, but evidence of preference for regulatory expansion over cost-benefit analysis.

3. **December 2024 FTB "Safety Net" hearing**: Used BOE platform to convene an activist hearing on income inequality, well outside the board's post-2017 mandate. Not a scandal but a documented tendency to treat the office as a policy advocacy platform rather than a narrow tax-administration body.

4. **Lost Santa Clara County supervisor race 2024**: While serving on BOE, she ran simultaneously for a different office and lost. Signals political ambition over focus; may reflect institutional commitment issues.

5. **Intra-party split**: Prominent Bay Area Democrats (Fiona Ma, Matt Mahan) backing opponent over her — reasons undisclosed in available sources. Warrants note as a signal of some establishment dissatisfaction, but cannot score without knowing rationale.

No documented ethics violations, corruption, or institutional norm-violations found in public record through May 2026.
