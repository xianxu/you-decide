# William Shireman — 2026 CA Board of Equalization D2

## Background

Bill Shireman is a San Francisco-based Republican, entrepreneur and environmental-business coalition-builder. UC Berkeley journalism graduate who taught leadership and negotiations at UC Berkeley's Haas Business School. Founded Future 500 (1996), a nonprofit bringing business and environmental advocates together. His signature prior achievement: brokering California's 1986 "bottle bill" recycling deposit system by uniting an "unlikely coalition of Coors, Safeway, and the Sierra Club" — a genuine cross-ideological coalition win. Officer of the Lincoln Club of Northern California; county GOP central committee member; former state GOP delegate. ([BayAreaGOP — profile](https://bayareagop.com/boe-candidate-bill-shireman-works-to-bring-opponents-together/) — Tier C, party-affiliated/candidate-friendly; biographical) Lost the 2022 CA Assembly District 17 Republican primary to incumbent Matt Haney and David Campos. ([KRON4 — "Meet the Republican trying to woo SF voters"](https://www.kron4.com/news/bay-area/meet-the-republican-trying-to-woo-sf-voters/) — Tier B)

## Stated positions

Ballot designation "Taxpayer Advocate"; self-described "fiscal conservative with a conscience." ([CalMatters — BOE voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/board-of-equalization/) — Tier B) Per his official candidate statement, the BOE platform is: ([CA SoS Voter Information Guide — BOE candidate statements](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/candidates/boe-candidate-statements.htm) — Tier A)

1. Ensuring taxes are administered competently and transparently
2. Protecting Proposition 13 while modernizing it
3. Defending taxpayers from unfair assessments
4. Promoting housing affordability and opportunity as a tax-reform goal
5. Credits "a self-funded recycling program" that "recycled over half a trillion cans, plastics, computers and more, with zero reliance on tax dollars"

He has separately framed the office's value in terms of its "soft power" — "extraordinary power that I intend to tap" — to pursue broader tax reform beyond the BOE's post-2017 narrow mandate. ([Indivisible Ventura — BOE D2 analysis](https://indivisibleventura.org/state-board-of-equalization-member-district-2-2026-primary-election/) — Tier B, hostile/progressive source, quoting his questionnaire)
