# William Shireman — 2026 CA Board of Equalization D2

## Background

Bill Shireman is a San Francisco-based Republican, 60s, entrepreneur and environmental-business coalition-builder. UC Berkeley journalism graduate. Founded Future 500 (1996), a nonprofit bringing business and environmental advocates together; currently also CEO of Solution Citizen consultancy. Taught leadership/negotiations at UC Berkeley Haas Business School. Published seven books. His signature prior achievement: brokering California's 1986 "bottle bill" recycling deposit system by uniting Coors, Safeway, and the Sierra Club — a genuine cross-ideological coalition win. Officer of the Lincoln Club of Northern California; county GOP central committee member; former state GOP delegate. Lost 2022 CA Assembly District 17 Republican primary.

## Stated positions

Self-described "fiscal conservative with a conscience" and "taxpayer advocate" (ballot designation). BOE platform:

1. Fair tax administration — uniformity, transparency, efficiency
2. Protect Proposition 13 while reducing taxes on housing construction and investment
3. Shift tax burden from "goods" (work, construction) to "bads" (pollution, waste, speculation)
4. Defend taxpayers from unfair assessments; housing affordability as tax-reform goal
5. Use BOE's "soft power" and convening role to put "honest solutions on the table" — explicitly values the office's broad influence beyond its post-2017 narrow mandate

## Record / past performance
