# John Pimentel — 2026 CA Board of Equalization D2

## Background

Democrat. Menlo Park resident. First-generation college graduate (San Joaquin Delta Community College → UC Berkeley → Harvard MBA). Career spans private-sector clean energy/infrastructure and government service.

**Government:** San Mateo County Community College District Trustee (led free-tuition initiative for 33,500+ county residents); California Deputy Secretary for Transportation (highway rebuild contracts, CHP headcount expansion, merged State Police into CHP); Menlo Park Housing Commissioner (promoted parking-lot-to-housing conversion downtown).
