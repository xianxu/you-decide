# Jim Irizarry — 2026 SM County Assessor / Clerk-Recorder / Chief Elections Officer

## Background

Age 74, Woodside. Puerto Rican immigrant to San Mateo County as a child, grew up in a working family. BA in Economics, San José State University. Career path: SM County social worker → HR Director, City of Redwood City → 13+ years as Assistant Assessor-County Clerk-Recorder and Assistant Chief Elections Officer under Mark Church (who is not running again). Board-Certified Property Tax Appraiser. 30+ years total in public sector.

He is the internal succession candidate — Church's direct deputy running to continue the office's established operating model.

## Record / past performance (Assistant Assessor tenure — this is the substrate)

This is the strongest institutionalist case in the race. Irizarry's record is the office's record:

- **35 elections administered** — including three presidential and three gubernatorial cycles under his oversight
- **13 assessment rolls closed** as deputy head of the assessor function
- **Led California's first all-mail election in 2015** — San Mateo County was first in the state to implement the vote-by-mail model
- Implemented the Assessor Property Assessment System (APAS) — major technology modernization
- Led cybersecurity initiatives and voter-accessibility expansions (disability access, non-English speakers, youth and senior outreach)
- ACRE earned "over a dozen local, state, national and international awards" for innovation and customer service under his leadership
- Board-Certified Property Tax Appraiser credential — direct professional qualification for the assessor function

No documented instances of election-integrity violations, certification challenges, or institutional norm-breaking in his tenure. The office under Church/Irizarry has operated cleanly through multiple contentious election cycles.
