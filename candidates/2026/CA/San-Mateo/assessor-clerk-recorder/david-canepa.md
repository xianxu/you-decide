# David Canepa — 2026 SM County Assessor / Clerk-Recorder / Chief Elections Officer

## Background

Fourth-generation San Mateo County resident. Daly City City Council → Mayor of Daly City. Elected to SM County Board of Supervisors District 5 in 2017; re-elected 2020; currently in third term (term-limited off by 2028). Ran in 2018 Democratic primary for CA-14 Congress (lost to Jackie Speier). Has overseen county's $5.5B budget and ~5,800 employees as supervisor.

**Career-move context:** Canepa is term-limited off the Board of Supervisors by 2028. Incumbent Mark Church (30+ years in public office, assessor since 2010) chose not to seek re-election. Canepa entered January 2026. The timing is transparently term-limit-driven — he leaves his seat two years early if elected. He frames it as answering a "dumpster fire" in the office, which is a substantive claim (backed by pending lawsuit against opponent Irizarry) but the structural incentive is evident.
