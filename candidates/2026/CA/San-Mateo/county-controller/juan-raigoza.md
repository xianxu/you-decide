# Juan Raigoza — 2026 SM County Controller

## Background

Career public servant, spent entire professional life in fiscal/audit roles. Prior to County: tax auditor at CA Franchise Tax Board; state & local tax consultant at Ernst & Young; management consultant (public sector) at Deloitte & Touche. Joined SM County Controller's Office in 2001 as senior internal auditor. Rose through payroll and information systems division management, then Assistant Controller (appointed 2012). Elected Controller January 2015 — has held office continuously since. Running for a fourth term in 2026.

Education: BS in Business Administration (finance/accounting concentration) and MBA (MIS concentration), both from Cal State Chico. **No CPA license mentioned on official profile or campaign materials** — professional memberships are GFOA, California State Association of County Auditors, and AGA (Association of Government Accountants). Note: the 2014 qualification challenge turned in part on whether his credentials satisfied the statutory "auditor or chief assistant auditor" requirement; courts found the language ambiguous and ruled in his favor.

Challenged in June 2026 primary by Thomas Royal Morgan II (no ballot designation; CPA-licensed but no public statement, campaign site, or documented audit/government experience surfaced — see `thomas-morgan.md` for the challenger profile). [Tier A: smcgov.org; Tier B: SM Daily Journal]

## Controversies / liabilities

**2014 ballot qualification challenge:** Opponent Joe Galligan (CPA, former Burlingame mayor) sued to keep Raigoza off the 2014 ballot, arguing his credentials did not satisfy the statutory "auditor or chief assistant auditor for at least three years" requirement. Superior Court ruled the statutory language "ambiguous" and cleared Raigoza; First District Court of Appeal denied Galligan's petition. Raigoza served as Assistant Controller since 2012 — two-year tenure, not three. Courts resolved this in his favor on statutory ambiguity grounds, not a clean credential win. [Tier B: SM Daily Journal, The Almanac, 2014]

Practical implication: Raigoza does not hold a CPA.
