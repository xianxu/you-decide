# Sam Liccardo — 2026 US House CA-D16

## Background

Sam Liccardo, born 1970, is the incumbent Democratic representative for CA-16 (Silicon Valley: San Jose, Palo Alto, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Mountain View, Santa Clara). Elected November 2024 succeeding Anna Eshoo. Prior career: felony prosecutor (Santa Clara County DA; federal SDCA), San Jose City Councilmember (2007–2014), San Jose Mayor (2015–2022). Education: Georgetown (BA), Harvard Kennedy School (MPA), Harvard Law (JD). First-generation college student; Portuguese-American. Ran against Evan Low in 2024 primary; won general easily in D+20 district.

2026 primary opponents: Kevin Johnson (R), Peter Soule (R), Jotham Stein (independent). This is a safe Democratic seat. Top-2 outcome is overdetermined — Liccardo advances with no viable Democratic challenger. The expressive-primary rule from `calibration-skills/expressive-primary-strategic-general.md` is not in tension here because there is no credible progressive challenger.
