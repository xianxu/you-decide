# Tony Thurmond — 2026 CA Governor candidate

## Background

Tony Krajewski Thurmond, born August 21, 1968 in Monterey, CA. Mother — a Panamanian immigrant — died when he was six; father did not return from Vietnam service. Raised by relatives in Philadelphia in a Hebrew Pentecostal household (a Black tradition incorporating Jewish observances). Grew up in poverty on public assistance and free school lunches. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Thurmond))

Education: BA from Temple University (was student body president); dual master's degrees from Bryn Mawr College in social policy and social work. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Thurmond))

Career arc: social worker → nonprofit director → school board → state assembly → Superintendent of Public Instruction. Mid-2000s: directed Beyond Emancipation (youth transitioning from child welfare/juvenile justice, Alameda County). Later managed programs at Golden Gate Regional Center (developmental disabilities) and Lincoln Child Center. Served on Richmond City Council. Seven bids for elected office between 2004–2017; won West Contra Costa School Board (2008), CA State Assembly (2014, 2016). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Thurmond))

State Assembly (CA-15, 2014–2018): Chaired Labor and Employment Committee; bills on court fine credits, methane emissions, immigrant defendant due process, tobacco restrictions near schools, prison-to-school funding shifts. Introduced 2017 resolution calling for Congressional censure of Trump after Charlottesville. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Thurmond))

Won 2018 Superintendent race over Marshall Tuck (charter school advocate) 50.9%–49.1%. Re-elected 2022 with 63.7%. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Thurmond))

Announced gubernatorial candidacy September 26, 2023. Resides in Richmond, CA; has two daughters. Describes himself as Jewish-adjacent ("a convert who's never been through a formal conversion"), works with Rabbi Rebekah Stern at Congregation Beth El in Berkeley on Holocaust education. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Thurmond))

## Stated positions

All positions from Thurmond's own campaign materials and candidate statements unless noted.

**Taxes / Wealth:** Proposes a tax credit for working- and middle-class Californians funded by a one-time 5% tax on the ~200 California residents with net worth over $1 billion (California Billionaire Tax Act). Also floated broader asset tax on those with >$150M. The asset-tax-on-$150M+ mechanism is the most specific in the field; Steyer separately backs the wealth-tax ballot initiative — both candidates are on the redistributive side. ([CalMatters 2026 voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/governor/); [American Community Media](https://americancommunitymedia.org/politics/tony-thurmond-stakes-out-progressive-flank-of-california-gubernatorial-race/))

**Housing:** Build 2 million housing units by opening surplus school district-owned land in every county for development; down-payment assistance grants. ([KEYT interview May 2026](https://keyt.com/news/california/2026/05/15/from-educator-to-governor-candidate-tony-thurmond-outlines-vision-for-california/))

**Healthcare:** Backs single-payer (cites personal loss — brother lost job, lost insurance, developed rare liver disorder). Supports using billionaire tax to backfill federal Medi-Cal cuts. ([CalMatters 2026 voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/governor/); [American Community Media](https://americancommunitymedia.org/politics/tony-thurmond-stakes-out-progressive-flank-of-california-gubernatorial-race/))

**Childcare:** Universal childcare program, framed as relieving working parents and supporting women/women-of-color-led small businesses. ([American Community Media](https://americancommunitymedia.org/politics/tony-thurmond-stakes-out-progressive-flank-of-california-gubernatorial-race/))

**Immigration:** First major gubernatorial candidate to call for dismantling ICE; pledged to "work with Congress to abolish ICE and to create a better system." ([American Community Media](https://americancommunitymedia.org/politics/tony-thurmond-stakes-out-progressive-flank-of-california-gubernatorial-race/))

**Education:** Frames it as "the great equalizer." Touts: universal preschool for four-year-olds, free school meals, $4B for student mental health, $6B broadband infrastructure (as Superintendent). ([KEYT interview May 2026](https://keyt.com/news/california/2026/05/15/from-educator-to-governor-candidate-tony-thurmond-outlines-vision-for-california/))

**Homelessness / Affordability:** Monthly tax credits to offset cost of living; no detailed homelessness plan surfaced in research (undocumented as of this writing).

## Record / past performance

### Superintendent of Public Instruction (2019–present)

**Powers are limited by design:** California's Superintendent cannot directly control schools; "local control" means districts run themselves. The office sets policy direction, administers state/federal funding, and uses its platform to pressure policy. Credit and blame must be weighted accordingly. ([CalMatters 2022](https://calmatters.org/education/2022/06/tony-thurmond-california-schools/))

**Test scores (mixed picture):**
- Post-COVID 2021–22: less than 50% of CA students proficient in ELA or math; math proficiency fell 7 points from 2019–20. ([ABC7 / KMPH coverage](https://abc7news.com/post/california-students-standardized-test-scores-declining-student-superintendent-tony-thurmond/12371918/))
- Thurmond declined to condemn pandemic school closures despite dismal scores; "It's hard to say" was widely quoted. ([KMPH](https://kmph.com/amp/news/local/california-official-wont-say-if-state-closed-schools-for-too-long-amid-dismal-test-scores-superintendent-tony-thurmond-covid-pandemic-learning-lossess-math-reading-ela-proficiency-florida-nyc))
- By 2025: 70.6% meeting ELA expectations, 61% math, 86% science — significant rebound, though announced by Newsom's office, not Thurmond's. Low-income, Black, and Latino students saw notable progress. ([CA Governor press release Oct 2025](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/10/09/governor-newsom-announces-california-student-test-scores-improve-significantly-signs-legislation-to-further-bolster-student-literacy-success/))

**Equity / access initiatives:**
- Digital divide initiative: distributed 100,000+ computers and hotspots, raised $30M. ([CalMatters 2022](https://calmatters.org/education/2022/06/tony-thurmond-california-schools/))
- Eight task forces on achievement gaps, literacy, technology access. ([CalMatters 2022](https://calmatters.org/education/2022/06/tony-thurmond-california-schools/))
- Legislation on teacher workforce diversity and student mental health services. ([CalMatters 2022](https://calmatters.org/education/2022/06/tony-thurmond-california-schools/))

**Union relationships:** Won 2018 race against charter-school-backed Tuck, signaling strong union alignment. CTA (California Teachers Association) has NOT endorsed Thurmond for governor — they shifted to Tom Steyer (announced April 14, 2026) after suspending support for Eric Swalwell over sexual assault allegations. ([CTA press release Apr 14 2026](https://www.cta.org/press-release/california-teachers-association-recommends-tom-steyer-for-california-state-governor); [EdSource Apr 15 2026](https://edsource.org/updates/california-teachers-association-endorses-tom-steyer-for-governor)) California Faculty Association gave Thurmond a dual endorsement (alongside Becerra). ([CFA endorsement](https://www.calfac.org/cfa-makes-dual-endorsement-for-california-governor/))

**Pandemic leadership:** Notably absent from a 2021 reopening press conference; counter-claimed it was isolated incident. Supporters say he worked behind the scenes. ([CalMatters 2022](https://calmatters.org/education/2022/06/tony-thurmond-california-schools/))

**Tensions with Newsom:** CalMatters noted Thurmond "was caught off guard by Newsom's plan to pare down the future scope of his job" — signals administrative friction rather than partnership. ([CalMatters 2026 voter guide](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/governor/))

### State Assembly record

Moderate-to-progressive legislative record: focused on labor rights, immigrant protections, youth justice pipeline, environmental regulation. No major fiscal controversies documented.

## Controversies / liabilities

**1. Toxic workplace (2021):** Politico spoke with 24 anonymous sources; nearly two dozen senior officials departed since 2019. Seven former officials said disagreement was not tolerated; three described the environment as "abusive" with mental health impacts. ([Politico Sep 28 2021](https://www.politico.com/states/california/story/2021/09/28/california-schools-chief-churns-through-top-aides-in-allegedly-toxic-workplace-1391461); [KVCR News](https://www.kvcrnews.org/local-news/2021-10-06/politico-report-accuses-state-education-superintendent-of-creating-a-toxic-workplace))

**2. Illegal residency hire (2021):** Hired longtime associate Daniel Lee as "superintendent of equity" while Lee was living in Philadelphia — violating California's state employee residency requirement. Initially funded through a Hewlett Foundation grant (~$179,832/yr), later converted to taxpayer funds. Lee resigned December 14, 2021. ([Politico Dec 11 2021](https://www.politico.com/states/california/story/2021/12/11/he-was-hired-to-fix-california-schools-while-running-a-business-in-philadelphia-1398697); [LAist/CalMatters summary](https://laist.com/news/education/what-has-tony-thurmond-done-as-california-schools-chief))

**3. Side gigs on public salary (2025):** While drawing a ~$200,000 superintendent salary, Thurmond reported working part-time for four different nonprofits since 2018. Most recently director of the Berkeley Food Pantry (earned $10,001–$100,000 in the last reported year). Unusual for a statewide elected official but not illegal. ([KQED](https://www.kqed.org/news/12034359/california-schools-chief-tony-thurmond-has-a-200000-salary-and-a-side-gig); [CalMatters Apr 2025](https://calmatters.org/politics/2025/04/schools-superintendent-side-jobs/))

**4. FPPC warning — PG&E behested payment:** Received warning from Fair Political Practices Commission for failing to timely report a $10,000 PG&E payment (at Thurmond's request) to Josie's Place grief program, where he was working as development manager at the time. ([CalMatters Apr 2025](https://calmatters.org/politics/2025/04/schools-superintendent-side-jobs/))

**5. Polling / viability:** Thurmond is a long-shot in the primary. Steyer has $132M to his ~$1.6M. CTA's snub is a structural signal. Low poll numbers have prompted questions about whether he can make the top-two runoff. ([Fox40](https://fox40.com/inside-california-politics/tony-thurmond-governor-race-polling/))

**6. ICE abolition position:** His call to abolish ICE is the most far-left position in the field and will draw attacks in a general. ([American Community Media](https://americancommunitymedia.org/politics/tony-thurmond-stakes-out-progressive-flank-of-california-gubernatorial-race/))
