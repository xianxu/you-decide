# Xavier Becerra — 2026 CA Governor candidate

## Background

Becerra, 68, is a lifelong California Democrat who represented a Los Angeles congressional district for 24 years (1993–2017). Governor Jerry Brown appointed him California's Attorney General in January 2017 — the first Latino to hold that office. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_Becerra) After four years as AG, President Biden nominated him as U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services (2021–2025), where he oversaw COVID vaccine distribution, drug-price negotiations under the Inflation Reduction Act, and major restructuring of HHS sub-agencies. [(Congress.gov bio)](https://www.congress.gov/117/meeting/house/112595/witnesses/HHRG-117-IF14-Bio-BecerraX-20210512.pdf) He announced his gubernatorial candidacy April 2, 2025.

As of late May 2026 he leads the field: the PPIC Statewide Survey (986 likely voters, fielded May 14–18, released May 28, 2026) put him first at **23%**, ahead of Steve Hilton (20%), Tom Steyer (15%), Chad Bianco (13%), and Katie Porter (12%). [(PPIC Statewide Survey, May 2026)](https://www.ppic.org/publication/ppic-statewide-survey-californians-and-their-government-may-2026/) [(NBC Los Angeles — PPIC poll, May 28 2026)](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/california-news/becerra-hilton-california-governor-election-poll/3896138/)

## Record / past performance

**As HHS Secretary (2021–2025):**
- Negotiated Medicare drug pricing for 10 high-cost drugs under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA), achieving 38%–79% discounts projected to save Medicare $6 billion annually; a second round of 15 drugs including Ozempic and Ibrance was announced January 17, 2025 (three days before Becerra left office). [(CMS — Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program, primary)](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/negotiating-lower-drug-prices-works-saves-billions) [(CMS — 15 additional drugs announcement, primary)](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/hhs-announces-15-additional-drugs-selected-medicare-drug-price-negotiations-continued-effort-lower)
- Oversaw record ACA Marketplace coverage expansion: HHS reported a then-all-time-high of more than 16 million Marketplace sign-ups for 2023 (later surpassing 21 million), with total ACA-related enrollment reaching ~40 million — ~9.3 million more than in 2021. [(CMS — record 21.3M ACA Marketplace coverage, primary)](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/historic-21-3-million-people-choose-aca-marketplace-coverage) The IRA also capped insured insulin out-of-pocket costs at $35/month for Medicare beneficiaries. [(HHS — action to lower insulin costs, primary)](https://www.hhs.gov/about/news/2023/03/1/statement-from-hhs-secretary-xavier-becerra-on-action-to-lower-insulin-costs.html)
- Oversaw COVID-19 vaccine distribution. Criticized for low public visibility during acute pandemic phases and for confusing federal public-health messaging. [(KQED, secondary — "What AG Becerra Reveals")](https://www.kqed.org/news/12084594/what-ag-xavier-becerra-reveals-about-a-possible-gov-becerra) *(visibility/messaging criticism is a characterization drawn from secondary coverage; the vaccine-distribution oversight itself is the HHS Secretary's statutory role.)*
- During his tenure, the FDA undertook a major reorganization — a unified Human Foods Program and restructured field operations — launched by FDA Commissioner Califf after the 2022 infant-formula crisis, approved May 2024 and implemented Oct 1, 2024. [(FDA — reorganization approved, primary)](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fdas-reorganization-approved-establishing-unified-human-foods-program-new-model-field-operations-and)

**As California AG (2017–2021):**
- Filed 122 lawsuits against the first Trump administration (per his campaign biography; KQED independently reported 120 at time of his 2025 gubernatorial announcement). [(xavierbecerra2026.com/bio, primary)](https://www.xavierbecerra2026.com/bio/) [(KQED, secondary)](https://www.kqed.org/news/12034105/xavier-becerra-enters-california-governors-race-citing-break-glass-moment)
- As AG, faced criticism from environmental advocates for perceived reluctance to aggressively litigate against oil companies. [(KQED, secondary, nonpartisan)](https://www.kqed.org/news/12083992/xavier-becerra-says-he-will-fight-for-california-who-did-he-fight-for-as-ag)
- Critics on the progressive side characterized his AG record as light on corporate accountability and policing reform. [(KQED)](https://www.kqed.org/news/12084594/what-ag-xavier-becerra-reveals-about-a-possible-gov-becerra)

**In Congress (1993–2017):**
- 24-year representative from Los Angeles; chaired the House Democratic Caucus. Standard liberal voting record; no anomalous votes surfaced in research.

## Controversies / liabilities

**1. Migrant children / HHS oversight failure (most serious):**
A February 8, 2024 HHS Office of Inspector General report (OEI-07-21-00250) documented systemic gaps in the Office of Refugee Resettlement's sponsor vetting and follow-up under Becerra's HHS: "In 16 percent of children's case files, one or more required sponsor safety checks lacked any documentation indicating that the checks were conducted," and "in 22 percent of cases, ORR did not conduct timely Safety and Well-Being Follow Up Calls, and in 18 percent of cases, the followup calls were not documented." [(HHS-OIG OEI-07-21-00250, Feb 8 2024, primary)](https://oig.hhs.gov/reports/all/2024/gaps-in-sponsor-screening-and-followup-raise-safety-concerns-for-unaccompanied-children/) Relatedly, roughly 85,000 unaccompanied migrant children could not be reached by phone after release to sponsors over 2021–2023; at a congressional hearing the ORR director could not answer questions about the figure. [(House Oversight Committee — ORR hearing wrap-up)](https://oversight.house.gov/release/hearing-wrap-up-orr-director-fails-to-answer-questions-about-85000-lost-unaccompanied-alien-children-flawed-vetting-of-sponsors-and-more%EF%BF%BC/) Becerra himself testified he was "unfamiliar" with the reported statistics. [(Fox News, right-tilt — flagged)](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/hhs-chief-unfamiliar-reports-agency-cant-contact-85000-unaccompanied-migrant-kids) Note: right-leaning outlets amplify this issue heavily and inflate it to "300,000 lost children"; the defensible figures are the OIG documentation-gap percentages and the ~85,000-unreachable count above.

**2. Campaign-account fraud (Becerra was the victim, not a participant):**
Dana Williamson — Gov. Newsom's former chief of staff and a veteran Democratic consultant — pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit bank/wire fraud and tax charges in a scheme that siphoned roughly $225,000 from Becerra's dormant state campaign account, funneling it to the family of Becerra's former federal deputy. Prosecutors stated Becerra was not involved and cooperated; the plea came the day of a major gubernatorial debate. Williamson agreed to $225,000 restitution to Becerra plus $500,000 to the IRS. [(CalMatters — Williamson plea deal, secondary)](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/05/california-newsom-chief-plea-deal/) [(CalMatters debate coverage, secondary)](https://calmatters.org/newsletter/xavier-becerra-last-governor-debate/)

**3. Oil money vs. climate record tension:**
Having accepted Chevron and CRC money via independent expenditures while running on a clean energy platform is being attacked by environmental groups and Steyer. CA Enviro Voters ran explicit opposition. [(EnviroVoters.org, partisan — environmental left)](https://envirovoters.org/becerra-is-wrong-about-big-oil/)

**4. Former Biden colleagues criticism:**
Some former Biden-era DOJ and HHS officials have publicly raised questions about his fitness and management style. The nature is characterized as concerns about accountability and management effectiveness rather than specific misconduct. [(NBC News, secondary)](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/xavier-becerra-pushback-biden-era-colleagues-california-governor-race-rcna344720)

**5. "Status quo" framing:**
At the final gubernatorial debate, San Jose Mayor Matt Mahan attacked Becerra as "the embodiment of the status quo," and Katie Porter pressed him on stage to disclose his state revenue plan. The broader framing — that his long establishment career makes him the avatar of Democratic governance failures — is the recurring line of attack against him. [(CalMatters — last governor debate, secondary)](https://calmatters.org/newsletter/xavier-becerra-last-governor-debate/)
