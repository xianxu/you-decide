# Katie Porter — 2026 CA Governor candidate

## Background

Katie Porter (born January 3, 1974, Fort Dodge, Iowa) grew up on a farm in the Midwest. She earned a BA from Yale University (1996, American studies) and a JD from Harvard Law School (2001), where she studied consumer law under Elizabeth Warren. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Porter)

After clerking for a federal appeals court and brief private practice, she became a tenured law professor at UC Irvine School of Law, where she founded the Consumer Protection Clinic. In 2012, California AG Kamala Harris appointed her to monitor bank compliance with the National Mortgage Settlement — the largest consumer settlement in US history. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Porter)

She is a single mother of three children.

**Congressional career (2019–2025):** Porter flipped California's 45th district (Orange County) in 2018, defeating Republican incumbent Mimi Walters 52–48%. Re-elected in 2020 and 2022. Served on House Oversight and Reform, Natural Resources, and Joint Economic committees. Deputy chair of the Congressional Progressive Caucus. Did not seek re-election to her House seat in 2024; ran instead for the US Senate, finishing third (15.3%) behind Adam Schiff and Steve Garvey in the March 2024 primary. [(GovTrack)](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/members/katie_porter/412758) [(CalMatters 2024)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2024/us-senate/katie-porter/)

Announced 2026 gubernatorial candidacy March 11, 2025. [(KatiePorter.com)](https://katieporter.com/)

## Record / past performance

**Whiteboard hearings:** Porter gained national recognition for using visual aids (whiteboards, charts) to pin down executives and officials during House Oversight hearings — Wells Fargo CEO on overdraft fees, CFPB director on payday loan math, pharmaceutical executives on stock buybacks vs. R&D. This is her signature brand. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Porter)

**Consumer protection:** Founded UC Irvine Consumer Protection Clinic; oversaw $18 billion National Mortgage Settlement compliance under AG Harris. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Porter)

**Votes:** Both Trump impeachments (yes). Progressive Caucus deputy chair. [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Porter)

**2024 Senate loss:** Lost to Adam Schiff decisively (Schiff ~39%, Porter ~15%). Used the word "rigged" about the primary, later walked it back: "I wish I had chosen a different word." [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Porter) [(CalMatters 2024)](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2024/us-senate/katie-porter/)

**Legislative output:** Detailed bill-passage record not reviewed in this research pass; known more for oversight and messaging than legislation passed.
