# Steve Hilton — 2026 CA Governor candidate

## Background

Stephen Glenn Charles Hilton was born August 25, 1969, in London, to Hungarian-Jewish parents who fled the 1956 Hungarian Revolution ([NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/who-is-steve-hilton-california-governor-election/3882164/)). His father was a notable European ice hockey player; after his parents divorced when he was five, he was raised by his mother in modest circumstances, receiving a bursary to Christ's Hospital School. He studied Philosophy, Politics, and Economics at New College, Oxford — the same path as most UK Conservative strategists.

**Cameron era (UK, 2010–2012):** Hilton worked at Conservative Central Office from the mid-1990s, befriending David Cameron there. He was Director of Strategy to Prime Minister Cameron from 2010–2012, authoring the party's 2010 manifesto and developing Cameron's "Big Society" agenda. He was satirized as Stewart Pearson in BBC's *The Thick of It*. Notably, he voted Green in 2001 before returning to the Conservative fold in 2005 and helping Cameron recast the Tories as centrist-green-progressive ([LA Times](https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-04-22/britain-steve-hilton-inspired-california)). His Cameron-era politics was UK center-right — meaningfully different from US MAGA conservatism.

**Move to California (2012 onward):** Hilton relocated to the Bay Area in 2012. He co-founded Crowdpac in 2014, a nonpartisan crowd-funding platform for political candidates ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2016/10/11/steve-hilton-crowdpac-founder-and-brexit-advocate-joins-disrupt-london-dec-5-6/)), resigning as CEO in 2018 ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/powerpost/wp/2018/05/18/fox-news-host-steve-hilton-wanted-to-disrupt-the-party-system-this-week-he-left-his-pac/)). He hosted *The Next Revolution* on Fox News from 2017–2023, where he branded himself a "positive populist."

**Naturalization:** He became a U.S. citizen in May 2021 and renounced his British citizenship in 2025, making him eligible to run for governor ([NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/who-is-steve-hilton-california-governor-election/3882164/)).

**Campaign launch:** He officially announced his candidacy on April 21, 2025, holding his launch event in Huntington Beach ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Steve_Hilton)).

## Stated positions

All positions drawn from his official campaign site ([stevehiltonforgovernor.com](https://stevehiltonforgovernor.com/)) and KPBS voter guide coverage ([KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/06/steve-hilton-lays-out-his-plans-to-make-the-state-califordable-as-governor)) unless noted. His framing slogan is "Califordable."

**Tax / fiscal:**
- No income tax on earnings under $100K; 7.5% flat tax above $100K. ([campaign site](https://stevehiltonforgovernor.com/))
- No property tax increases; explicit "Protect Prop. 13!" pledge.
- Return state spending to pre-pandemic levels (from current ~$322B budget).
- Regulatory sunset: all regulations expire unless explicitly renewed; agencies must remove existing rules before adding new ones.

**Housing:**
- Cap "impact fees" (which he says constitute ~20% of new home price).
- Eliminate anti-housing CEQA abuse (he claims ~70% of CEQA suits block housing).
- Oppose Coastal Commission and CARB restrictions on housing zones.

**Education:**
- Target 100% of students meeting math and English standards (current: 35% math, 47% English).
- "A grade for every school, a grade for every teacher"; fire worst-performing teachers.
- Restore parent-trigger laws.
- Expand charter schools and school choice.
- Oppose "social and political indoctrination"; protect parental rights on sex/gender questions.
- Oppose transgender girls competing in girls' sports.

**Energy / climate:**
- Self-identifies as "an environmentalist" but questions whether California climate policies "actually help the climate" or just inflict costs on families. ([KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/06/steve-hilton-lays-out-his-plans-to-make-the-state-califordable-as-governor))
- Goal: $3/gallon gas; cut electric bills in half.
- "Use the oil and gas we produce here rather than shipping it in on giant supertankers halfway around the world." (direct quote, [KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/06/steve-hilton-lays-out-his-plans-to-make-the-state-califordable-as-governor))

**Public safety / homelessness:**
- Full enforcement of existing laws (theft, drug markets, violent crime, immigration).
- $24B spent on homelessness "without any idea where it went" — enforcement over additional spending.
- Anti-sanctuary-state; cites Obama-era enforcement as model for consistent application of immigration law. ([KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/06/steve-hilton-lays-out-his-plans-to-make-the-state-califordable-as-governor))

**Self-framing:** "I'm a pragmatist; I'm not an ideologue, I'm not tribal." — direct quote. ([KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2026/05/06/steve-hilton-lays-out-his-plans-to-make-the-state-califordable-as-governor)) He identifies as "the candidate of the legal immigrant community." ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/republican-steve-hilton-california-governor-race-2026-donald-trump-11849699))

## Record / past performance

Hilton has never held elected office. His record is his media commentary (Fox News, 2017–2023) and policy advocacy. Consistent themes:

- **"Positive populism"** — a branded framing from his Fox show and his 2017 book *Positive Populism*. The thesis: mainstream parties have abandoned working people; insurgent economics (lower taxes, deregulation, local control) is the real populism.
- **COVID-era commentary:** Hilton was an early promoter of "the cure is worse than the disease" framing, advocating rapid reopening. ([PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2021/feb/08/worldnetdaily/no-dr-anthony-fauci-did-not-fund-research-tied-cov/))
- **Fauci/COVID origins conspiracy:** In 2021 he advanced unsubstantiated claims that Dr. Fauci commissioned work that created COVID-19 — rated false by PolitiFact. His Fox News segment (June 6, 2021) claimed "irrefutable evidence that Fauci lied about funding gain-of-function experiments." ([Fox News — Hilton segment](https://www.foxnews.com/media/hilton-video-dr-fauci-lied-gain-of-function-experiments-wuhan-lab-coronavirus); [PolitiFact fact-check](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2021/feb/08/worldnetdaily/no-dr-anthony-fauci-did-not-fund-research-tied-cov/)) *Note: Fox News link is Hilton's own framing; PolitiFact rates the underlying claim false.*
- **2020 election fraud claims:** As of May 2026 Hilton still refuses to state on the record that Biden won the 2020 election. When pressed five times by MSNBC hosts he called the question "ridiculous games" and did not answer directly. ([Mediaite, May 4, 2026](https://www.mediaite.com/media/tv/ms-now-hosts-throw-down-with-steve-hilton-after-he-refuses-to-say-who-won-2020-election-why-cant-you-answer-the-question/); [Fox News video — 2020 skepticism segment](https://www.foxnews.com/video/6217465801001))
- **Crowdpac (2014–2018):** Attempted to build nonpartisan political fundraising infrastructure. Resigned as CEO 2018; no significant documented scandal, but the venture did not scale.

## Controversies / liabilities

1. **UK political past vs. US populist persona:** Hilton helped design Cameron's centrist-green-progressive Tory rebranding (including voting Green in 2001). His current MAGA-adjacent populism is a substantial ideological journey. The Cameron era was UK establishment center-right; his current positioning as anti-establishment populist is a genuine discontinuity worth noting. ([LA Times, Apr 22 2026](https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-04-22/britain-steve-hilton-inspired-california))

2. **COVID Fauci conspiracy theory (2021):** Claimed Fauci commissioned COVID creation on his Fox News show — rated false by PolitiFact. He has not publicly walked this back. ([Fox News — Hilton segment, Jun 6 2021](https://www.foxnews.com/media/hilton-video-dr-fauci-lied-gain-of-function-experiments-wuhan-lab-coronavirus); [PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2021/feb/08/worldnetdaily/no-dr-anthony-fauci-did-not-fund-research-tied-cov/)) *Fox link is his own framing; PolitiFact rates it false.*

3. **2020 election refusal:** As of May 2026 Hilton refuses to confirm Biden won — five direct questions, no direct answer. ([Mediaite, May 4, 2026](https://www.mediaite.com/media/tv/ms-now-hosts-throw-down-with-steve-hilton-after-he-refuses-to-say-who-won-2020-election-why-cant-you-answer-the-question/))

4. **British citizenship renounced 2025:** Renounced to run for governor. Naturalized May 2021, renounced 2025 — timing is clearly career-driven. ([NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/who-is-steve-hilton-california-governor-election/3882164/))

5. **No governing experience:** Fox host and political strategist, never managed a public institution. The "great manager" case is unproven.

6. **Cash burn rate:** Raised $6.6M but already spent the majority entering the home stretch. ([CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/02/governors-race-fundraising-reports/))

7. **Party convention rejection (relative):** CA GOP delegates preferred Chad Bianco over Hilton — the base of the base is not consolidated behind him despite Trump's endorsement.
