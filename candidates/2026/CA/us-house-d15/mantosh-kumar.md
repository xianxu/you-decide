# Mantosh Kumar — 2026 US House CA-D15

## Background

Age 53, San Mateo resident for 25+ years. Electrical Engineering degree + MBA. Career in Silicon Valley corporate roles: Oracle (product engineering), Cisco (strategy/finance/execution), ServiceNow (strategy/operations). Claims management of programs valued $100M–$1B+. No prior elected or appointed office — first-time candidate. No documented civic leadership roles (boards, commissions, community orgs) surfaced in research. Wife works for the University of California.

Motivation for primarying Mullin: frustration that incumbent Democrats are "lying down — hoping that people's outrage will propel them to power" and insufficient action on housing, healthcare, corporate capture. Specifically criticizes Mullin's support for Prop 19 and an "Empty Lot" bill as developer-friendly. Also cites Mullin's AIPAC-linked donations (~$862,000 cited by Kumar) as evidence of capture.
