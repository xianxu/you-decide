# Kevin Mullin — 2026 US House CA-D15

## Background

San Mateo County native. South San Francisco City Council and Mayor before state legislature. California State Assembly 2012–2022, serving as Speaker Pro Tem 2014–2022; authored 60+ enacted bills including all-vote-by-mail and dark-money campaign-finance reform. Succeeded Jackie Speier in 2022 with 73%+ of the vote. Now seeking third term (2026). Prior private-sector: ran KM2 Communications, a South San Francisco video-production firm.

District: San Mateo County + Southeast San Francisco. Cook PVI D+26 — Democratic safe seat.

## Stated positions

From campaign site (kevinmullinforcongress.com — Tier A):
- Combat authoritarianism, defend democracy
- Healthcare affordability
- Housing cost reduction
- Climate action / clean energy jobs
- Reproductive rights
- Fiscal responsibility and fair taxation

Israel/Gaza: Claims to be "very critical" of Netanyahu's execution of the war. States he has not accepted AIPAC funds this cycle. (SM Daily Journal, Tier B)

## Controversies / liabilities

1. **PAC concentration / conflict-of-interest optics**: 56% PAC-funded, primarily from industries his committee regulates. Challenger Dang calls it "a direct conflict of interest and an ethical violation." This is the central attack line in the 2026 primary. (Almanac, SM Daily Journal — Tier B)

2. **Israel/Gaza record gap**: Verbally pro-ceasefire but did not sign the formal ceasefire letter; abstained on IHRA definition; voted against the $17.6B aid package. The record is mixed enough to draw criticism from both pro-Israel and pro-Palestinian sides. AJP Action score of 27% (out of 100%) signals he is viewed as insufficiently pro-Palestinian by that advocacy org.

3. **No notable independence from caucus**: Appears to be a reliable party-line vote. No documented case of voting against Democratic caucus on a high-profile measure.

4. **Not AIPAC-funded this cycle** (self-stated, SM Daily Journal — Tier B): Relevant given the district's Silicon Valley progressive lean and 2024 cycle national attention to AIPAC spending.
