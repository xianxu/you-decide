# Anthony Van Dang — 2026 US House CA-D15

## Background

Disabled Marine Corps combat veteran, Iraq; "I got shot, survived two IED blasts, and lost close friends." Brother Andrew killed in action in Ramadi, Iraq in 2004 — enlisted "the next day." Grew up in Foster City; lives in Belmont. Harvard MPA. Post-military Pentagon financial oversight, then defense contracting where he reported fraud ("I found problems with the entire industry and called them out. The retaliation cost me my career") — featured in a More Perfect Union exposé, "He Audited the Pentagon: Here's Where the Waste Really Is." ([dangforcongress.com](https://www.dangforcongress.com/), re-fetched live 2026-05-29)

No prior elected office. This is his first political campaign. Certified on the 2026 ballot as "Anthony Van Dang — Democratic, Policy Analyst" ([CA SoS Certified List of Candidates, June 2 2026 primary](https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf)).

## Stated positions

All from the campaign issues page ([dangforcongress.com/issues](https://www.dangforcongress.com/issues), Tier A; re-fetched live 2026-05-29 — page carries all positions below verbatim, no scrub rot).

**Israel/Gaza:** Calls Gaza "a genocide and the United States is funding it." Supports "an immediate and permanent ceasefire, recognition of a Palestinian state, restoration of UNRWA funding, and an arms embargo on any government under active investigation by the International Court of Justice." States "I take no money from defense contractors and no money from AIPAC."

**Immigration:** Supports "a real pathway to citizenship for undocumented residents," protections for DACA recipients and TPS holders. Opposes using federal agencies as "instruments of intimidation against our communities." Personal: father is a refugee.

**Federal taxes:** Progressive — "a wealth tax above a high threshold, a true minimum tax on the highest earners that includes unrealized capital gains, and an end to the step-up basis at death"; tax "executive stock and option compensation at the same rate as wages"; raise the federal minimum wage with regional cost-of-living adjustments.

**Healthcare:** "Healthcare is a right, not a revenue stream." Calls for "Medicare for All. Single-payer."

**Climate:** When a company causes an environmental disaster, "the executives who made the decisions should face criminal liability, not just corporate fines."

**Housing:** "Real renter protections, including federal support for just-cause eviction standards and rent stabilization"; targets corporate landlords / institutional investors.

**Workers:** End "the subminimum wage for tipped workers, for workers with disabilities, and for agricultural workers."

**Democracy:** Opposes voter suppression and gerrymandering. No specific institutional-defense record (no prior office).
