# Anthony Van Dang — 2026 US House CA-D15

## Background

Disabled Marine Corps combat veteran (2004–2008), second Battle of Ramadi survivor (gunshot, two bomb blasts). Lost brother Andrew in Iraq. Son of a Vietnam War refugee; mother is Mexican-Native American. Grew up in Foster City (district resident). Harvard MPA; attended UCSD and community college. Post-military: built veteran nonprofits, worked Pentagon financial oversight, moved to defense contracting where he reported waste/fraud and was fired — featured in a More Perfect Union exposé on defense-industry corruption. Lives in Belmont with wife Anne (also a Marine veteran) and two sons, one autistic.

No prior elected office. This is his first political campaign.

## Stated positions

**Israel/Gaza** (Tier A — campaign site): Calls Gaza "a genocide" funded by the US. Supports permanent ceasefire, Palestinian state recognition, UNRWA funding restoration, arms embargo on nations under ICC investigation. Explicitly rejects AIPAC and defense-contractor funding.

**Immigration** (Tier A): Supports pathway to citizenship for undocumented residents, DACA/TPS protections. Opposes "cruelty as deterrent." Personal: father is a refugee. Frames current ICE operations as "weaponization of federal agencies against our communities."

**Federal taxes** (Tier A): Progressive — wealth tax above high thresholds, eliminate step-up basis at death, tax executive stock comp at wage rates, end dynasty trusts, raise federal minimum wage tied to regional cost-of-living.

**Healthcare** (Tier A): "Healthcare is a right, not a revenue stream." No explicit Medicare-for-All language on site (contrast with Kumar who uses it explicitly).

**Climate** (Tier A): Aggressive decarbonization, criminal liability for executives causing environmental disasters, renewable infrastructure investment.

**Housing** (Tier A): Affordable housing federal incentives, just-cause eviction, rent stabilization, targets corporate landlords and institutional investors.

**Workers** (Tier A): Living wages, union protections, end subminimum tipped/agricultural wages.

**Democracy** (Tier A): Opposes voter suppression and gerrymandering. No specific institutional-defense record (no prior office).

No voting record (no prior elected office). Whistleblower record in defense contracting is the closest proxy for institutional-defense behavior — public-interest courage under pressure, at personal cost (fired). No controversies found in public record.
