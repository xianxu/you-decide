# Jeff Rosen — 2026 Santa Clara County District Attorney (Incumbent)

## Background

Jeff Rosen has served as Santa Clara County District Attorney since 2011 — 15 years running the largest prosecutorial office north of Los Angeles, serving a county of nearly two million people. He is nonpartisan by ballot designation. His lone challenger in the June 2 primary is Deputy DA Daniel Chung, a subordinate inside Rosen's own office. [(Ballotpedia)](https://ballotpedia.org/Jeff_Rosen)

## Stated positions / priorities

Sources from his official campaign site unless otherwise noted.

**Prosecution priorities:** Violent crime (murder, rape, domestic violence, organized retail theft/smash-and-grab), mental health diversion for low-level and mentally ill offenders, anti-trafficking, financial/political corruption. Frames approach as a "triangle" of vigorous prosecution + crime prevention + ethical standards. [(jeffrosen.org)](https://www.jeffrosen.org/the-triangle-of-criminal-justice-reform)

**Death penalty:** In 2020 adopted a categorical policy of not seeking the death penalty in any case — a notable unilateral departure that drew police-union opposition. Cited personal moral evolution after visiting the National Memorial for Peace and Justice in Montgomery, AL. [(KTVU)](https://www.ktvu.com/news/santa-clara-county-da-wont-seek-death-penalty-san-jose-police-union-fights-back)

**Mental health diversion:** Claims SCC was among the first counties to operate mental health courts with dedicated judges focused on treatment-not-incarceration for mentally ill defendants in low-level cases. Supports diversion programs for non-violent first offenders. [(jeffrosen.org/guestions)](https://www.jeffrosen.org/guestions) [(Davis Vanguard — 2022 debate)](https://davisvanguard.org/2022/05/santa-clara-da-jeff-rosen-trips-challenger-after-first-in-person-debate/)

**Drug / incarceration reform:** Office has offered expungements for low-level drug convictions; jail population reduction as a stated reform. No documented ICE-hold or immigration-enforcement posture found in research — SCC is a sanctuary-adjacent county; Rosen has not made immigration enforcement a priority issue.

**Conviction integrity:** Created a first-of-its-kind "Conviction Integrity Unit" (later renamed Public and Law Enforcement Integrity Unit) with: Brady Committee investigating police misconduct, double-blind eyewitness identification reforms, and Open File Discovery. [(da.santaclaracounty.gov)](https://da.santaclaracounty.gov/faq/about-district-attorney) Also applauded access to police misconduct records under SB 1421. [(San José Spotlight)](https://sanjosespotlight.com/santa-clara-county-da-applauds-high-court-ruling-giving-prosecutors-access-to-police-misconduct-records/)

## Record highlights

**Prosecutions that defined the tenure:**
- **Brock Turner (2016):** Prosecuted and obtained conviction of Stanford swimmer for sexual assault of unconscious woman. Opposed the lenient six-month sentence from Judge Aaron Persky but did not appeal (defended as legally appropriate). Instead lobbied Sacramento; the resulting CA law eliminates suspended sentences in comparable cases. Endorsed Persky's retention in the subsequent recall — controversial given the case. [(Palo Alto Online)](https://www.paloaltoonline.com/news/2017/04/10/district-attorney-endorses-judge-facing-recall/)
- **George Shirakawa:** Prosecuted former SCC Supervisor for public-funds theft.
- **Michael Tyree:** Prosecuted three jail correctional officers who beat to death a mentally ill inmate.
- **Nuestra Familia gang:** Multi-count murder and drug-trafficking prosecution.
- **Sierra Lamar case:** Kidnapper/killer of 15-year-old.
- **Sex trafficking:** Ongoing prosecutorial unit.

**Office conduct and internal friction (most serious record concerns):**
- **Daniel Chung reinstatement refusal:** Chung was reinstated by independent arbitrator and county Personnel Board after a disciplinary dispute, but Rosen refused to assign him prosecutorial work for years, paying him ~$314,600 from 2022–2025 to perform no duties. Lawsuit filed April 2026. Palo Alto Daily Post: "DA sued for paying prosecutor not to work." [(Davis Vanguard)](https://davisvanguard.org/2026/04/prosecutor-paid-not-work/) [(Palo Alto Daily Post)](https://padailypost.com/2026/04/13/da-sued-for-paying-prosecutor-not-to-work/)

- **Brady disclosure practice:** ACLU sued for withholding records on racial bias in prosecution (Nov 2025). [(Davis Vanguard ACLU piece)](https://davisvanguard.org/2025/11/aclu-lawsuit-prosecutorial-transparency/)

**Budget posture:** In March 2026, op-ed in Davis Vanguard accused Rosen of threatening the public to protect his office budget during a historic county deficit. [(Davis Vanguard)](https://davisvanguard.org/2026/03/santa-clara-county-budget-cuts/)

## Controversies / liabilities

**1. Stanford pro-Palestinian protesters — recusal (dominant controversy, 2025–2026):**
Rosen charged 12 Stanford students with felony vandalism and conspiracy (April 2025) after a June 2024 protest in which students occupied the university president's office to protest Gaza killings. The case ended in a mistrial (Feb 13, 2026). For retrial, a judge ordered full recusal of Rosen's office after evidence emerged that:
- Rosen's campaign website framed the prosecution as "fighting antisemitism"
- A December 2025 campaign email blast to 600+ Los Angeles supporters linked to the case on his campaign site
- Rosen publicly called the students "un-American"

SCC Superior Court Judge Kelley Paul found the conflict "so grave as to render it unlikely that the defendants would receive fair treatment." The state AG's office may now take over retrial. This is an anti-hypocrisy red flag: using an active prosecution to fundraise for reelection is a bright-line violation of the independence norm for the DA's office. [(KQED)](https://www.kqed.org/news/12082713/santa-clara-county-da-barred-from-retrying-pro-palestinan-stanford-protesters) [(Bolts)](https://boltsmag.org/santa-clara-da-jeff-rosen-recused-stanford-student-palestine-prosecutors-retrial/) [(Palo Alto Online)](https://www.paloaltoonline.com/santa-clara-county/2026/05/07/santa-clara-county-district-attorneys-office-recused-from-stanford-felony-trial/) [(Stanford Daily)](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/04/30/jeff-rosen-fundraising-records/)

**2. Brock Turner / Judge Persky recall endorsement:**
While prosecution itself was appropriate and outcome positive (new CA law), Rosen's decision to endorse the lenient-sentencing judge Aaron Persky during the recall campaign drew significant backlash from victim advocates. The anti-hypocrisy axis is activated: championed the prosecution, then protected the judge. [(Palo Alto Online 2017)](https://www.paloaltoonline.com/news/2017/04/10/district-attorney-endorses-judge-facing-recall/)

**3. Personal feud driving race framing:**
Palo Alto Daily Post reporting (May 13, 2026) characterizes the Rosen–Chung race as partly driven by a personal feud, with Chung being the subordinate Rosen refused to assign work. This cuts against impartial stewardship of the office. [(Palo Alto Daily Post)](https://padailypost.com/2026/05/13/personal-feud-colors-race-for-da/)

**4. Recall Rosen movement:**
An organized RecallRosen.org campaign exists, citing prosecutorial misconduct and office culture concerns. [(recallrosen.org)](https://www.recallrosen.org/)
